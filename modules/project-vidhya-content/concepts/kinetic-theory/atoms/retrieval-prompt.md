**Question:** State the kinetic theory pressure equation and the temperature-energy relation. Then state the three typical molecular speeds, in increasing order.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Kinetic theory pressure equation:** $PV=\dfrac{1}{3}Nm\langle v^2\rangle$.

**Temperature-energy relation:** $\dfrac{1}{2}m\langle v^2\rangle=\dfrac{3}{2}kT$ (mean translational kinetic energy per molecule).

**Typical speeds, smallest to largest:** most probable speed $v_p=\sqrt{2RT/M}$ < average speed $v_{avg}=\sqrt{8RT/(\pi M)}$ < rms speed $v_{rms}=\sqrt{3RT/M}$.

</details>
