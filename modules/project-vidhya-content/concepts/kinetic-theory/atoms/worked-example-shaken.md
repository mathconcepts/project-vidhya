**Problem:** Same sealed cylinder: $n=2$ mol helium, $M=0.004$ kg/mol, $T=300$ K, $V=0.05\ \text{m}^3$. Find rms speed, total internal energy, pressure.

---

**Step 1 — What "monatomic" fixes.** Helium has only $f=3$ degrees of freedom (motion in $x$, $y$, $z$). No rotation term to add on top.

---

**Step 2 — rms speed, one number at a time.** $3RT=3(8.314)(300)=7482.6$. Divide by $M=0.004$: $7482.6/0.004=1{,}870{,}650$. Take the square root: $v_{rms}\approx1368$ m/s.

---

**Step 3 — Internal energy.** $nRT=(2)(8.314)(300)=4988.4$. Multiply by $\frac{3}{2}$: $U=7482.6$ J.

---

**Step 4 — Pressure.** Reuse $nRT=4988.4$ from Step 3. Divide by $V=0.05$: $P=99768$ Pa.

---

**Step 5 — Check the pressure makes sense.** $99768$ Pa is close to one atmosphere ($101325$ Pa) — a perfectly reasonable pressure for a lab cylinder, not an absurd number.
