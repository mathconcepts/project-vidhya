What is $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\frac{2^x-1}{x}$?

- **(A)** $1$
- **(B)** $\ln 2$
- **(C)** $2$
- **(D)** The limit does not exist

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. The standard limit $\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{a^x-1}{x}=\ln a$ applies with $a=2$, giving $\ln 2\approx0.693$. This is the one member of the standard-limits family that does **not** equal $1$ — every other one on the list does, which is exactly why it is the one students most often get wrong under time pressure.

</details>
