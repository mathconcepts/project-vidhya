- **Forgetting to check endpoints:** On a closed interval $[a,b]$, the global max/min might be at an endpoint, not at a critical point.
- **Confusing local and global:** A local max at $x = c$ might not be a global max.
- **Inconclusive second derivative test:** When $f''(c) = 0$, students give up. Use the first derivative test instead.
