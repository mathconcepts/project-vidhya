- **Wrong order in product rule:** Students write $(u'v)$ instead of $(u'v + uv')$.
- **Quotient rule denominator:** Students forget to square $v$ in the denominator or mess up the sign (should be $u'v - uv'$, not $uv' - u'v$).
- **Not simplifying:** Students leave factorization opportunities, making the answer look wrong.
