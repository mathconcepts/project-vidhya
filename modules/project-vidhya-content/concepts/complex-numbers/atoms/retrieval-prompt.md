From memory, before checking: how do you compute the modulus $|a + bi|$, and what does multiplying by a complex number do geometrically?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$|a + bi| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$ (e.g. $|3 - 4i| = 5$). Multiplying by a complex number scales by its magnitude and rotates by its angle — complex multiplication is rotation + scaling.
</details>
