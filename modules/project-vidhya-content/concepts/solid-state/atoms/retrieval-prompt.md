**Question:** State $Z$, the atom-touching relationship, and the packing efficiency for simple cubic, bcc, and fcc.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- **Simple cubic**: $Z=1$, $a=2r$, packing efficiency $\approx52.4\%$.
- **bcc**: $Z=2$, $\sqrt{3}a=4r$, packing efficiency $\approx68\%$.
- **fcc**: $Z=4$, $\sqrt{2}a=4r$, packing efficiency $\approx74\%$.

</details>
