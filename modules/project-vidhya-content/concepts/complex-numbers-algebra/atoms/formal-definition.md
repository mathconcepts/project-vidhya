**Complex number**: $z = a+bi$, with $a,b$ real and $i^2=-1$. $a=\text{Re}(z)$, $b=\text{Im}(z)$.

**Argand plane**: $z=a+bi$ is plotted as the point $(a,b)$, real axis horizontal, imaginary axis vertical.

**Modulus**: $|z| = \sqrt{a^2+b^2}$, the distance of the point from the origin.

**Argument**: $\theta = \arg(z)$, the angle the line joining the origin to $z$ makes with the positive real axis, measured anticlockwise. The **principal argument** is the value with $-\pi < \theta \le \pi$.

**Polar form**: $z = r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)$, where $r=|z|$ and $\theta=\arg(z)$.

**Multiplication in polar form**: if $z_1=r_1(\cos\theta_1+i\sin\theta_1)$ and $z_2=r_2(\cos\theta_2+i\sin\theta_2)$, then $z_1z_2 = r_1r_2\big(\cos(\theta_1+\theta_2)+i\sin(\theta_1+\theta_2)\big)$ — moduli multiply, arguments add.

**De Moivre's theorem**: for any integer $n$, $(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^n = \cos(n\theta)+i\sin(n\theta)$.

**Cube roots of unity**: the three solutions of $z^3=1$: $1$, $\omega = \cos120°+i\sin120° = -\tfrac{1}{2}+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}i$, and $\omega^2 = \cos240°+i\sin240° = -\tfrac{1}{2}-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}i$. They satisfy $\omega^3=1$ and $1+\omega+\omega^2=0$.
