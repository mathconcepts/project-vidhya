**Question:** State the **Spectral Theorem** for real symmetric matrices. What does it guarantee about eigenvalues and eigenvectors?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**Spectral Theorem:** Let $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ be a symmetric matrix. Then:

1. **All eigenvalues of $A$ are real.**
2. **Eigenvectors corresponding to distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal.**
3. **There exists an orthonormal basis of eigenvectors:** equivalently, $A$ can be written as $$A = Q\Lambda Q^T$$ where $Q$ is an orthogonal matrix (columns are orthonormal eigenvectors) and $\Lambda$ is a diagonal matrix of eigenvalues.

</details>
