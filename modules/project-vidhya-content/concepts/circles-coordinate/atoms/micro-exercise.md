Find the equation of the chord of contact of tangents drawn from $(7,1)$ to the circle $x^2+y^2-2x-4y-4=0$.

**(A)** $6x-y-13=0$
**(B)** $6x+y-13=0$
**(C)** $x-6y-13=0$
**(D)** $6x-y+13=0$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**(A).**

Compare to $x^2+y^2+2gx+2fy+c=0$: $g=-1$, $f=-2$, $c=-4$.

Chord of contact: $T\equiv xx_1+yy_1+g(x+x_1)+f(y+y_1)+c=0$ at $(x_1,y_1)=(7,1)$.

$7x+y-(x+7)-2(y+1)-4=0 \Rightarrow 7x+y-x-7-2y-2-4=0 \Rightarrow 6x-y-13=0$.

</details>
