Tell a pilot "fly 500 km" and they'll ask "which way?" A number alone — 500 km — is a **scalar**. "500 km north-east" is a **vector**: it carries both size and direction. Force, velocity, displacement — almost everything that moves or pushes in engineering needs a direction attached to its magnitude, and vector algebra is the toolkit for combining and comparing them.
