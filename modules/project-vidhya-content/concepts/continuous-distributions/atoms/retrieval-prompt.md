From memory: state the memoryless property of the exponential distribution, and name which other distribution on this topic also satisfies it.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$P(X>s+t\mid X>s)=P(X>t)$. No other continuous distribution on this topic (Normal, Uniform, Gamma) satisfies it — memorylessness is unique to Exponential here.
</details>
