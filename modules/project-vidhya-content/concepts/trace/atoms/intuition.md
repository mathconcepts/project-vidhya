Trace is just "add the diagonal entries." Simple to compute — but why is that simple number so useful? Watch what happens when you look at the same transformation from a different set of axes.

That's the real reason trace is worth checking: it doesn't care how you set up your coordinates (your choice of axes). Two people, two different axes, same matrix underneath — they'll always compute the same diagonal sum. Use that as a free check whenever you find eigenvalues by hand: add them up, compare to the diagonal, and a mismatch means recheck your arithmetic.
