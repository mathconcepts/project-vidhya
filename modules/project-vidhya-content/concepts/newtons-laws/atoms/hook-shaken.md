A block $m_1=3$ kg sits on a frictionless table. A string over a pulley connects it to a hanging block $m_2=2$ kg. Take $g=10$ m/s$^2$.

Settle the sign convention separately for each block, before any equation. For $m_1$: positive is "toward the pulley." For $m_2$: positive is "downward." Both blocks share the same acceleration magnitude, each read positive in its own chosen direction.

Draw two free-body diagrams. $m_1$: only the tension $T$ pulls it, so $T=m_1a$. $m_2$: gravity $m_2g$ pulls it down, tension $T$ pulls it up, so $m_2g-T=m_2a$.

Two equations, two unknowns — $T$ and $a$ — solved together next.
