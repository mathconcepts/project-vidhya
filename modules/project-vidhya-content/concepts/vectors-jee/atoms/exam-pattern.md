**How JEE Main actually asks this.**

- **MCQ: area or volume via a triple product.** "Find the area of the triangle/parallelogram with sides..." or "find the volume of the tetrahedron/parallelepiped with vertices...". Both reduce to one cross product and, for the volume version, one dot product on top of it. Recognise the shape word — triangle needs a $\frac12$ factor, parallelogram does not; tetrahedron needs a $\frac16$ factor, parallelepiped does not.

- **MCQ: "using vectors, show that..." is a vector-proof question in disguise.** It almost always collapses to the midpoint formula, $\frac{\vec a+\vec b}{2}$, or to showing a scalar triple product is zero for collinearity/coplanarity. Do not reach for coordinates — the whole point of the question is that vectors settle it in two or three lines.

- **NAT: a numeric value of $\vec a\cdot\vec b$, $|\vec a\times\vec b|$, or a scalar triple product.** Pure computation, no interpretation needed — the risk is entirely arithmetic and sign slips, not concept.

- **Trap: coplanar is not the same claim as parallel.** A scalar triple product of $0$ proves the three vectors lie in one plane. It says nothing about any pair of them being parallel.

- **Time budget:** under 60 seconds for a single dot or cross product with integer components; under 100 seconds for a scalar triple product, including writing out the $3\times3$ determinant.
