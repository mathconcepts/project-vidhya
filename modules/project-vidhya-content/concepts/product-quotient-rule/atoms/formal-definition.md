**Product Rule**: If $f(x) = u(x) \cdot v(x)$, then:
$$f'(x) = u'(x) \cdot v(x) + u(x) \cdot v'(x)$$

**Quotient Rule**: If $f(x) = \frac{u(x)}{v(x)}$, then:
$$f'(x) = \frac{u'(x) \cdot v(x) - u(x) \cdot v'(x)}{[v(x)]^2}$$
