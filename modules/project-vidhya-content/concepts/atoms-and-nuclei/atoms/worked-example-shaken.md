**Problem:** Find the binding energy per nucleon of helium-4 ($_2^4\text{He}$). Given: $m_H=1.007825$ u, $m_n=1.008665$ u, $M(\text{He-4})=4.002603$ u, $1$ u $=931.5$ MeV$/c^2$.

---

**Step 1 — Count protons and neutrons.** $A=4$, $Z=2$, so neutrons $=A-Z=2$.

---

**Step 2 — Add the separate masses.** $2(1.007825)+2(1.008665)=2.01565+2.01733=4.03298$ u.

---

**Step 3 — Subtract the actual nucleus mass.** $\Delta m=4.03298-4.002603=0.030377$ u.

---

**Step 4 — Multiply by the conversion factor.** $BE=0.030377\times931.5=28.30$ MeV.

---

**Step 5 — Divide by $4$ nucleons.** $BE/A=28.30/4=7.07$ MeV per nucleon.

$$\boxed{BE\approx28.30\text{ MeV},\quad BE/A\approx7.07\text{ MeV per nucleon}}$$

Answer: $BE\approx28.30$ MeV, binding energy per nucleon $\approx7.07$ MeV.
