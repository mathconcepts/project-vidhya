**How GATE actually asks this.**

- **MCQ questions** typically ask for the numeric value of a single partial derivative at a given point.
- **NAT questions** commonly ask for $dz/dt$ via the multivariable chain rule along a parametrized path, evaluated at a specific $t$.
- **MSQ questions** may combine partial derivatives with critical-point conditions (setting both partials to zero) and ask which statements about the resulting point are true.

**Worked numeric pattern.** $\partial/\partial x\,(xy^2)$ at $(2,3)$: treat $y^2$ as constant, giving $y^2=9$.

**Time budget.** About $2$ minutes for a single partial derivative at a point; $3$–$4$ minutes for a full chain-rule or Jacobian question.
