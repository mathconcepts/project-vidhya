How many subgroups does the group $(\mathbb{Z}_8, +)$ have?

- **(A)** 2
- **(B)** 4
- **(C)** 8
- **(D)** 3

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. By Lagrange's theorem, subgroups of $\mathbb{Z}_8$ have orders that divide $|\mathbb{Z}_8| = 8$. Divisors of 8 are: 1, 2, 4, 8. Since $\mathbb{Z}_8$ is cyclic, there is exactly ONE subgroup for each divisor of 8. The four subgroups are: $\{0\}$ (order 1), $\{0,4\}$ (order 2, generated by 4), $\{0,2,4,6\}$ (order 4, generated by 2), and $\{0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7\}$ (order 8, the whole group). So there are 4 subgroups total.
