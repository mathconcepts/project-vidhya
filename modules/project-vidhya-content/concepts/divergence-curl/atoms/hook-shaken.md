Look at the field $\mathbf{F}=(x,0,0)$ at $(1,0,0)$: flow speeds up as $x$ grows, so more leaves a small box there than enters — a source. Now look at $\mathbf{F}=(-y,x,0)$ at the origin: it turns $(1,0)$ toward $(0,1)$ without ever pushing outward — pure rotation. The first is divergence; the second, curl.
