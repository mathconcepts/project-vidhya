Geometrically, a quadratic form $f(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{x}^T A \mathbf{x}$ describes the shape of level curves in space. If $A$ is positive definite, these curves are ellipsoids (the bowl opens up in all directions). If $A$ is indefinite, you get a saddle shape—positive in some directions, negative in others. The eigenvalues of $A$ control the 'width' of the bowl along each principal axis. In 2D, you've already seen this: $x^2 + y^2 = 1$ is an ellipse, but $x^2 - y^2 = 1$ is a hyperbola (saddle).
