Let $A=\{1,2,3\}$. Write out every pair $(a,b)$ with $a,b\in A$ and $a\le b$: $(1,1),(1,2),(1,3),(2,2),(2,3),(3,3)$ — six pairs, listed by hand. That list is a relation on $A$. Which of reflexive, symmetric, transitive does this particular list actually have?
