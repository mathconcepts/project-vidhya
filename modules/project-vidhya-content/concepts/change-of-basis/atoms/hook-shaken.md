Take $x=(3,1)$ in standard coordinates.

Basis $B=\{(1,1),(1,-1)\}$. Solve $a(1,1)+b(1,-1)=(3,1)$: $a+b=3$ and $a-b=1$, so $a=2$, $b=1$.

$[x]_B=(2,1)$ — same point, different pair of numbers, because the basis changed.
