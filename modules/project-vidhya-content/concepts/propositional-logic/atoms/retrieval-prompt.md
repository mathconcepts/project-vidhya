From memory, before checking: of $P\to Q$'s converse, inverse, and contrapositive, which one is guaranteed to share its truth value?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Only the contrapositive, $\neg Q\to\neg P$. The converse ($Q\to P$) and inverse ($\neg P\to\neg Q$) are logically independent of the original.
</details>
