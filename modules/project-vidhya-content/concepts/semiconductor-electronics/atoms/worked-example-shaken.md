**Problem:** A silicon photodiode has band gap $E_g\approx1.1$ eV. Find the maximum wavelength of light it can detect.

---

**Step 1 — State what "just barely detects" means.** A photon's energy must be at least $E_g$ to lift an electron across the gap. The longest wavelength possible is where photon energy exactly equals $E_g$.

---

**Step 2 — Write the photon-energy formula.** $E\approx\dfrac{1240}{\lambda(\text{nm})}$ eV.

---

**Step 3 — Set $E=E_g$ and solve.** $\dfrac{1240}{\lambda_{max}}=1.1 \Rightarrow \lambda_{max}=\dfrac{1240}{1.1}\approx1127$ nm.

---

**Step 4 — Check against visible light.** Visible light stops around $700$ nm. $1127$ nm is well past that, in the infrared — so this diode also picks up infrared, not only visible light.

$$\boxed{\lambda_{max}\approx1127\text{ nm}}$$

Answer: $\lambda_{max}\approx1127$ nm.
