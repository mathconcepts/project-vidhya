An elevator's position over time never jumps — it cannot vanish from the third floor and reappear on the fifth without passing through the fourth. That is continuity: no holes, no jumps. Now suppose the elevator hits the bottom, stops dead, and reverses direction instantly, with no slowing down first. Its position-versus-time graph has a sharp corner right at that instant, like a tick mark. Predict, before reading on: at that exact moment, does the elevator have a single well-defined speed? Its position was never discontinuous — yet something about its motion has genuinely broken down there, and that something is a stricter, separate question from continuity.
