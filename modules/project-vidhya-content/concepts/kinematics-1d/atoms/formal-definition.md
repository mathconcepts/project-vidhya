**Displacement** $s$: position measured from a fixed origin along a chosen positive direction (a vector in one dimension — its sign carries the direction).

**Average velocity** over an interval: $\bar{v} = \dfrac{\Delta s}{\Delta t}$. **Instantaneous velocity**: $v = \dfrac{ds}{dt}$.

**Average acceleration** over an interval: $\bar{a} = \dfrac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}$. **Instantaneous acceleration**: $a = \dfrac{dv}{dt}$.

**Equations of motion** (constant acceleration $a$, initial velocity $u$, velocity $v$ at time $t$, displacement $s$):
$$v = u + at, \qquad s = ut + \tfrac{1}{2}at^2, \qquad v^2 = u^2 + 2as.$$

**Displacement in the $n$-th second** (constant acceleration): $s_n = u + \dfrac{a}{2}(2n-1)$.

**Free fall convention**: taking upward as positive, an object under gravity alone (no air resistance) has $a=-g$, where $g \approx 9.8$ m/s$^2$ (often taken as $10$ m/s$^2$ for quick JEE arithmetic) points downward regardless of which way the object is moving.

**Relative velocity in one dimension**: for two objects $A$ and $B$ moving along the same line with velocities $v_A$ and $v_B$ (measured in the same frame, same sign convention), the velocity of $A$ relative to $B$ is $v_{AB} = v_A - v_B$.
