$\int \frac{dx}{x^2-1}$ looks hopeless, right up until you notice that $\frac{1}{x^2-1} = \frac{1/2}{x-1} - \frac{1/2}{x+1}$. Now it is two logarithms and you are done. Partial fractions is the systematic way to find that split — and it is the same manoeuvre that makes inverse Laplace transforms tractable.
