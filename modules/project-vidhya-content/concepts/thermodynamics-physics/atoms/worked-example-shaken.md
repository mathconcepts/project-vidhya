**Problem:** Same Carnot engine: $T_1=600$ K, $T_2=300$ K, absorbs $Q_1=1000$ J per cycle. Find efficiency, work output, and heat rejected.

---

**Step 1 — Write down the ratio first.** $T_2/T_1=300/600=0.5$.

---

**Step 2 — Efficiency.** $\eta=1-0.5=0.5$, i.e. $50\%$.

---

**Step 3 — Work, one multiplication.** $W=\eta Q_1=0.5\times1000=500$ J.

---

**Step 4 — Heat rejected, one subtraction.** $Q_2=Q_1-W=1000-500=500$ J.

---

**Step 5 — Check the answer makes sense.** $Q_2=500$ J is less than $Q_1=1000$ J — some heat had to be rejected, by the second law, and it was.
