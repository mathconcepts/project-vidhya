**Problem:** $C_1=2\ \mu\text{F}$ charged to $V_1=6\ \text{V}$, disconnected. $C_2=4\ \mu\text{F}$ charged to $V_2=12\ \text{V}$, disconnected. Positive plates joined together, negative plates joined together. Find the final common voltage and the energy lost.

---

**Step 1 — Find $Q_1$.** $Q_1=C_1V_1=2\times6=12\ \mu\text{C}$.

---

**Step 2 — Find $Q_2$.** $Q_2=C_2V_2=4\times12=48\ \mu\text{C}$.

---

**Step 3 — Add the charges (like plates were joined).** $Q_{\text{total}}=12+48=60\ \mu\text{C}$.

---

**Step 4 — Add the capacitances (they now share both nodes).** $C_1+C_2=2+4=6\ \mu\text{F}$.

---

**Step 5 — Divide to get the shared voltage.** $V=\dfrac{60}{6}=10\ \text{V}$.

$$\boxed{V = 10\ \text{V}}$$

---

**Step 6 — Check by comparing energy.** Before: $\tfrac12(2)(6)^2+\tfrac12(4)(12)^2=36+288=324\ \mu\text{J}$. After: $\tfrac12(6)(10)^2=300\ \mu\text{J}$. Energy dropped by $24\ \mu\text{J}$ — this is expected, not an error, since some energy always leaves as heat when charge redistributes.
