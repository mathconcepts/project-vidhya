**Problem:** Write the IUPAC name of $[Co(NH_3)_5Cl]Cl_2$.

---

**Step 1 — Split the formula.** Complex ion (inside the brackets): $[Co(NH_3)_5Cl]$. Ions outside: $2$ chloride ions.

---

**Step 2 — Work out the charge on the complex ion.** The compound overall is neutral, and the $2$ chloride ions outside carry $2 \times (-1) = -2$. So the complex ion must carry $+2$ to balance it: $[Co(NH_3)_5Cl]^{2+}$.

---

**Step 3 — Find the metal's oxidation state.** Inside the brackets: $5$ ammonia ligands are neutral ($5 \times 0 = 0$); $1$ chlorido ligand carries $-1$. For the whole complex ion to equal $+2$: (oxidation state of Co) $+ 0 + (-1) = +2$, so cobalt's oxidation state is $+3$.

---

**Step 4 — Name each ligand type.** Ammonia as a ligand is "ammine" (neutral ligand, keeps a special name). Chloride as a ligand is "chlorido" (anionic ligand, $-o$ ending).

---

**Step 5 — Add prefixes and order alphabetically.** $5$ ammine ligands become "pentaammine"; $1$ chlorido ligand stays "chlorido". Alphabetically, "ammine" comes before "chlorido", so ammine is named first regardless of the prefix in front of it.

---

**Step 6 — Assemble the full name.** Ligands (alphabetical), then metal name with oxidation state in Roman numerals, then the counter-ion name last. $\boxed{\text{pentaamminechloridocobalt(III) chloride}}$.
