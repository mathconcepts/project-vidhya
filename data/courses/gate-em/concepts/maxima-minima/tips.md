# Teaching Tips: Maxima & Minima

## Common Student Errors

- **Forgetting to check endpoints:** On a closed interval $[a,b]$, the global max/min might be at an endpoint, not at a critical point.
- **Confusing local and global:** A local max at $x = c$ might not be a global max.
- **Inconclusive second derivative test:** When $f''(c) = 0$, students give up. Use the first derivative test instead.

## GATE Question Pattern

GATE asks: (1) find critical points (MCQ or NAT), (2) classify as max/min (MCQ), (3) applied optimization problems (NAT, 2 marks). Piecewise functions and constrained optimization are common.

## Speed Tricks for MCQs

- **Always check $f'' > 0$ → min, $f'' < 0$ → max** (if $f'(c) = 0$).
- **Endpoints matter:** On closed intervals, evaluate $f$ at critical points AND endpoints.
- **Applications:** In word problems, express the quantity to optimize in terms of one variable, then use calculus.

## Must-Memorize Formulas / Results

- **Critical point:** $f'(c) = 0$ or $f'(c)$ DNE.
- **First derivative test:** $f'$ changes sign at $c$ → local extremum.
- **Second derivative test:** If $f'(c) = 0$ and $f''(c) \\neq 0$, then $f''(c) > 0$ → min, $f''(c) < 0$ → max.
- **Global extrema on $[a,b]$:** Evaluate $f$ at all critical points in $(a,b)$ and at endpoints $a, b$.
