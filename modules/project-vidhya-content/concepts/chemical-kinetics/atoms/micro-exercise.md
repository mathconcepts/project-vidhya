The decomposition of $\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$ is first order with $k=3.47\times10^{-3}\ \text{min}^{-1}$. What is its half-life?

A) $\approx 200\ \text{min}$
B) $\approx 100\ \text{min}$
C) $\approx 3.47\ \text{min}$
D) $\approx 693\ \text{min}$
E) Cannot be found without the initial concentration

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct answer: A) $\approx 200\ \text{min}$.**

$t_{1/2}=\dfrac{0.693}{k}=\dfrac{0.693}{3.47\times10^{-3}}\approx 199.7\ \text{min}\approx200\ \text{min}$.

**Why the others fail:**
- B) $100\ \text{min}$ comes from using $t_{1/2}=\dfrac{0.5}{k}$ by mistake — forgetting the $\ln2\approx0.693$ factor and using $0.5$ instead.
- C) $3.47\ \text{min}$ mistakes $k$ itself (in different units) for the half-life.
- D) $693\ \text{min}$ comes from forgetting to divide $0.693$ by $k$ — treating $0.693$ alone, in the wrong units, as the answer.
- E) is wrong precisely because first-order half-life does NOT depend on the initial concentration — that is the one fact this question is testing.

</details>
