One equation, $\frac{dy}{dx}+y=1$, has many solution curves. The starting point $(0,0)$ picks out one of them.

Read the equation directly: $\frac{dy}{dx}=1-y$. When $y$ is small, $1-y$ is close to $1$ — fast growth. When $y$ gets close to $1$, $1-y$ gets close to $0$ — growth almost stops. So the curve rises fast at first, then flattens out near $y=1$, never quite reaching it.
