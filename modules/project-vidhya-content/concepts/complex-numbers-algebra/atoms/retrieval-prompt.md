**Question:** State De Moivre's theorem. Then state the three cube roots of unity and the one identity relating all three of them.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**De Moivre's theorem:** for any integer $n$, $(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^n = \cos(n\theta)+i\sin(n\theta)$.

**Cube roots of unity:** $1$, $\omega=-\dfrac12+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}i$, $\omega^2=-\dfrac12-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}i$.

**Identity:** $1+\omega+\omega^2=0$ (and $\omega^3=1$).

</details>
