**How this unit is asked, when it does appear.**

- **MCQ: classify this polymer.** Given a monomer or pair of monomers, the question is really asking "does joining these release a small molecule, or not?" — check that single fact before anything else.

- **MCQ: name the monomer(s) of a commercially named polymer.** Nylon-6,6, Buna-S, Buna-N, and PET are recurring names; each has one specific, fixed monomer or monomer pair to recall directly.

- **Trap: assuming more cross-linking always means more elastic.** A lightly cross-linked rubber is elastic; a heavily cross-linked thermoset like Bakelite is rigid and brittle instead. The amount of cross-linking, not merely its presence, decides the outcome.

- **Trap: assuming "two monomers" always means condensation.** Buna-S and Buna-N are addition copolymers of two different monomers — the release test, not the monomer count, decides the category.
