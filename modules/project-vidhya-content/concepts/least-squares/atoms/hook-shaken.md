This matrix, $\begin{pmatrix}0.8&0.4\\0.4&0.2\end{pmatrix}$, is a projection. Feed in $(2,1)$: it lands right back on $(2,1)$ — unmoved. Feed in $(1,-2)$: it lands at $(0,0)$ — gone. Every other arrow lands somewhere between, on the line through $(2,1)$. That "closest point on the line" move is least squares.
