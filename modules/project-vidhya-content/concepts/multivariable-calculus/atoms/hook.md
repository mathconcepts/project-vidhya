A hillside's steepness isn't one number — it depends on which direction you walk. Walk due east and the ground might climb steeply; walk due north from the very same spot and it might barely rise at all. A **partial derivative** answers one specific version of that question: freeze every direction except one, and ask how fast the height changes along just that one. Freeze the WRONG value of the frozen variable, though, and you're measuring the slope of a completely different slice of the hill — a mistake that looks identical to the right answer until you check the numbers.
