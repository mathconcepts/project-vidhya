Watch what a matrix does to every direction at once. This one squashes the whole plane down onto a single line — every arrow ends up somewhere on that line, no matter where it started. Only one direction survives at full strength; the direction running the other way gets crushed all the way to nothing. One kept, one killed, and they always add up to the whole space — that's rank plus nullity.
