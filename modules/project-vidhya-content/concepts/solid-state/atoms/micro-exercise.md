An element crystallises in a face-centred cubic (fcc) unit cell. How many atoms are present per unit cell, and what is the coordination number?

A) $Z=4$, coordination number $12$
B) $Z=4$, coordination number $8$
C) $Z=2$, coordination number $12$
D) $Z=1$, coordination number $6$
E) $Z=4$, coordination number $6$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct answer: A) $Z=4$, coordination number $12$.**

fcc has $8$ corner atoms ($8\times\frac{1}{8}=1$) plus $6$ face-centred atoms ($6\times\frac{1}{2}=3$), giving $Z=1+3=4$. Its coordination number — the tightest possible packing for identical spheres — is $12$.

**Why the others fail:**
- B) $8$ is bcc's coordination number, wrongly paired with fcc's $Z$.
- C) $Z=2$ is bcc's atom count, wrongly paired with fcc's coordination number.
- D) $Z=1$, coordination number $6$ describes simple cubic entirely, not fcc.
- E) pairs the right $Z$ with simple cubic's coordination number instead of fcc's own $12$.

</details>
