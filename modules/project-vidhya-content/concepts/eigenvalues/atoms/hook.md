Push an arrow through a matrix and it usually comes out pointing somewhere new — turned and stretched at once. But every matrix hides a few stubborn directions: arrows it can stretch but can never turn. Those stubborn arrows are the eigenvectors, and their stretch factors are the eigenvalues. Sixteen arrows are about to go through $\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$ — spot the two that refuse to turn.
