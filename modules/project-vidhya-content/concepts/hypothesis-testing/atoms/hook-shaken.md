1000 hours claimed. 50 bulbs average 980 — a 20-hour gap. Is 20 hours a real shortfall, or could random bulb-to-bulb variation alone produce a 20-hour swing from a true 1000-hour average? Hypothesis testing turns that either/or into a number.
