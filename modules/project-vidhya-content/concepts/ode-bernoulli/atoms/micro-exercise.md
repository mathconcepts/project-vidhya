Identify the Bernoulli equation and state its order $n$: $\frac{dy}{dx} - \frac{y}{x} = y^3$

- **(A)** $n = 1$ (linear)
- **(B)** $n = 2$
- **(C)** $n = 3$
- **(D)** $n = -1$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**. The Bernoulli form is $\frac{dy}{dx} + P(x)y = Q(x)y^n$.

Rewrite: $\frac{dy}{dx} - \frac{y}{x} = y^3$.

Here, $P(x) = -\frac{1}{x}$, $Q(x) = 1$, and the power on the right side is $n = 3$.

This is a genuine Bernoulli equation (not linear, since $n \neq 0, 1$). The answer is $n = 3$.

</details>
