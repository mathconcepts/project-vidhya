A beam's deflection, a circuit's power draw, a projectile's height — engineering keeps asking for the largest or smallest value some quantity can take. At a smooth interior peak or valley the tangent goes flat, so the derivative is zero, and "find the best" becomes "solve an equation". The marks are lost at the points where it does not: endpoints, and corners where no derivative exists.
