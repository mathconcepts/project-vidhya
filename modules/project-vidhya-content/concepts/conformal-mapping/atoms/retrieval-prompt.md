From memory, before checking: what is the local magnification factor at $z=2+i$ under the conformal map $f(z)=z^3$?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$15$. Magnification $=|f'(z)|$; $f'(z)=3z^2$, so $f'(2+i)=3(2+i)^2=3(3+4i)=9+12i$, and $|9+12i|=\sqrt{81+144}=\sqrt{225}=15$. (Equivalently $3|2+i|^2=3\cdot5=15$.)
</details>
