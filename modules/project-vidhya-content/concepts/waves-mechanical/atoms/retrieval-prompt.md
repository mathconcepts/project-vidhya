**Question:** State the wave equation relating speed, frequency, and wavelength. Then state the Doppler effect formula and what makes each sign positive or negative.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Wave equation:** $v=f\lambda$.

**Doppler effect:** $f'=f\dfrac{v\pm v_o}{v\mp v_s}$. Numerator: $+$ for an observer moving toward the source, $-$ for moving away. Denominator: $-$ for a source moving toward the observer, $+$ for moving away.

</details>
