**Question:** State the actual (Aufbau-defying) electron configurations of chromium and copper, and state the spin-only magnetic moment formula.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Chromium:** $[Ar]3d^54s^1$ (not $3d^44s^2$) — half-filled $3d$ plus half-filled $4s$ is extra stable.

**Copper:** $[Ar]3d^{10}4s^1$ (not $3d^94s^2$) — fully filled $3d$ plus half-filled $4s$ is extra stable.

**Spin-only magnetic moment:** $\mu = \sqrt{n(n+2)}$ BM, where $n$ is the number of unpaired electrons.

</details>
