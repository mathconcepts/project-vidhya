**"Pick the equation missing what you don't have."** The three equations of motion each omit exactly one of $s,u,v,a,t$: $v=u+at$ has no $s$; $s=ut+\tfrac{1}{2}at^2$ has no $v$; $v^2=u^2+2as$ has no $t$. Match your missing, unwanted quantity to the equation that already left it out.

**"Slope tells you push, area tells you place."** On a velocity-time graph: the *slope* at a point is the acceleration (how hard the "push" is at that instant); the *area* between the curve and the time axis, over an interval, is the displacement (how far the object actually got).

**"Fix the sign before you touch a number."** Decide upward-positive or downward-positive (or left/right, whichever the problem is about) as the very first step, on paper, before substituting anything — the sign convention is not optional bookkeeping, it is the thing that makes every later $+$ or $-$ mean something.
