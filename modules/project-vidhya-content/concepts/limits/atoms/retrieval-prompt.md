Before checking, try to recall from memory: what two conditions must both hold before L'Hôpital's rule may be applied to $\lim f(x)/g(x)$?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>The form must be $\frac00$ or $\frac{\infty}{\infty}$ at the point in question, **and** $f,g$ must be differentiable near that point. Applying the rule to a fraction that isn't indeterminate gives a wrong answer, not an alternative correct one.</details>
