**Question:** State the discriminant condition for each of the three types of roots of $ax^2+bx+c=0$. Then state the sum and product of roots formulas.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Let $D = b^2-4ac$.

- $D>0$: two real, distinct roots.
- $D=0$: one real root, repeated.
- $D<0$: two complex-conjugate roots, no real roots.

**Sum of roots:** $\alpha+\beta = -\dfrac{b}{a}$.
**Product of roots:** $\alpha\beta = \dfrac{c}{a}$.

</details>
