**How JEE actually asks this.**

- **NAT: "find the coefficient of $x^k$" or "find the term independent of $x$."** Identify $a$ and $b$ correctly (sign included), write the general term, and solve the exponent equation for $r$. This is almost always a two-line answer once $r$ is found.

- **MCQ: identify the middle term(s).** Check the parity of $n$ first — that alone tells you whether to expect one middle term or two, before touching the formula.

- **NAT: greatest term for a specific numeric value of the variable.** This needs the ratio test $T_{r+1}/T_r$, not the greatest-coefficient formula — plug in the given value and track where the ratio crosses $1$.

- **Trap: greatest coefficient vs. greatest term.** These sound alike and are tested precisely because they are not the same question. "Greatest coefficient" ignores the values of $a$ and $b$; "greatest term" does not.

- **NAT: divisibility or remainder application.** Rewrite the base as (a multiple of the divisor) $\pm\,1$ raised to some power, expand, and keep only the one surviving term.

- **Time budget:** under $90$ seconds for a general-term or middle-term question; up to $2$ minutes for a greatest-term or remainder-application question, since both need one extra reasoning step before the arithmetic starts.
