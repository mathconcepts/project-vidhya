What is the spin-only magnetic moment of $Cr^{3+}$ ($3d^3$)?

A) $1.73$ BM
B) $2.83$ BM
C) $3.87$ BM
D) $4.90$ BM
E) $5.92$ BM

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct answer: C) $3.87$ BM.**

$Cr^{3+}$ has 3 electrons in 5 $d$ orbitals. With only 3 electrons and 5 available orbitals, all 3 go into separate orbitals unpaired — this happens regardless of ligand field strength, since there are not enough electrons yet to force any pairing. So $n=3$.

$\mu = \sqrt{n(n+2)} = \sqrt{3 \times 5} = \sqrt{15} \approx 3.87$ BM.

**Why the others fail:**
- A) $1.73$ BM corresponds to $n=1$ (one unpaired electron, like $Cu^{2+}$).
- B) $2.83$ BM corresponds to $n=2$.
- D) $4.90$ BM corresponds to $n=4$ (like high-spin $Fe^{2+}$).
- E) $5.92$ BM corresponds to $n=5$ (like $Mn^{2+}$ or high-spin $Fe^{3+}$).

</details>
