Touch a silk scarf, then touch a cotton shirt. Different feel, different chemistry.

Silk is a protein — a long chain of **amino acid** units joined end to end.

Cotton is cellulose — a long chain of **glucose** units joined end to end instead.

Same idea both times (small units, strung into a long chain), different building block. That one question — which small unit is this chain built from? — is the whole of this concept.
