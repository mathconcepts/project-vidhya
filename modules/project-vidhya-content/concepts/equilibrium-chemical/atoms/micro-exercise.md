For $A(g) \rightleftharpoons B(g)$, $K_c = 4.0$. At a given instant, $[A]=1.0$ M and $[B]=1.0$ M. Which statement is correct?

A) $Q=1$; the reaction is already at equilibrium
B) $Q=1$; the reaction proceeds forward, toward $B$
C) $Q=4$; the reaction proceeds in reverse, toward $A$
D) $Q=1$; the reaction proceeds in reverse, toward $A$
E) Cannot be determined without the volume of the container

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct answer: B) $Q=1$; the reaction proceeds forward, toward $B$.**

$Q = \dfrac{[B]}{[A]} = \dfrac{1.0}{1.0} = 1$. Since $Q(1) < K_c(4.0)$, the reaction still needs to move forward, making more $B$, before it reaches equilibrium.

**Why the others fail:**
- A) The reaction is at equilibrium only when $Q=K_c$; here $Q=1 \ne 4 = K_c$, so it is not.
- C) $Q=4$ is simply wrong — the correct computed value is $1$, not $4$.
- D) $Q=1$ is correctly computed here, but the direction is backwards: $Q<K_c$ means forward, not reverse.
- E) Both concentrations are already given in molarity (mol/L); no volume conversion is needed to compute $Q$.

</details>
