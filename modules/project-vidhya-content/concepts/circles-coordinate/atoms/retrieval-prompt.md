From memory, before checking: what ONE condition on $(x_1,y_1)$ must hold before the equation $T=0$ actually describes a real chord of contact, rather than just a line?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The point must be genuinely outside the circle: $S_1=x_1^2+y_1^2+2gx_1+2fy_1+c>0$. Without that, $T=0$ still spits out a straight line — but with no real tangents from $(x_1,y_1)$ behind it, so nothing real actually touches the circle at either end of that line.

</details>
