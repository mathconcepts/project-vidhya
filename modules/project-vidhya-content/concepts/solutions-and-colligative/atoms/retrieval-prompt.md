**Question:** State all four colligative properties with their formulas, and state what the van't Hoff factor $i$ physically represents.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- Relative lowering of vapour pressure: $\dfrac{p^\circ-p_s}{p^\circ}=i\,x_{\text{solute}}$
- Boiling-point elevation: $\Delta T_b=iK_bm$
- Freezing-point depression: $\Delta T_f=iK_fm$
- Osmotic pressure: $\Pi=iCRT$

$i$ is the actual number of particles produced in solution per formula unit dissolved — $1$ for a non-electrolyte, an integer $n$ for a strong electrolyte splitting into $n$ ions, and a value between $1$ and $n$ (or even below $1$, for association) otherwise.

</details>
