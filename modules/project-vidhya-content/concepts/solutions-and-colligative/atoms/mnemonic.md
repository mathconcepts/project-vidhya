**"Colligative Counts, never Cares who."** Every colligative property runs off particle COUNT alone — it never cares which specific substance is dissolved.

**"Vapour wants a Fraction; Boiling and Freezing want a Kilogram; Osmotic wants a Litre."** A quick reminder of which concentration unit belongs to which formula: mole FRACTION for vapour-pressure lowering, molality (per KILOGRAM of solvent) for $\Delta T_b$/$\Delta T_f$, molarity (per LITRE of solution) for $\Pi$.

**"$i=1$ is a claim, not a default."** Never assume $i=1$ silently — state it as a deliberate claim (non-electrolyte, or a stated assumption) before using it in any formula.
