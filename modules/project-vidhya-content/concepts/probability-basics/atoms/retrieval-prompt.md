From memory, before checking: write Bayes' theorem in terms of prior $P(A)$, likelihood $P(B\mid A)$, and evidence $P(B)$.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$P(A\mid B) = \dfrac{P(B\mid A)\,P(A)}{P(B)}$ — posterior equals likelihood times prior, divided by evidence.
</details>
