"$D>0$ means positive profit exists somewhere" reads as a complete answer on a shop's profit parabola. It is not — $D>0$ only guarantees profit is positive **between the two roots**, and says nothing about whether that price range is sensible to actually charge.

If the roots of $-2x^2+40x-150=0$ work out to $x=5$ and $x=15$, profit is positive only for prices raised between ₹5 and ₹15 above cost — not for every price, and not automatically the best one either. A positive discriminant is necessary for profit to ever be positive; it is nowhere near sufficient to tell you the shop is doing well at the price it actually charges.
