Two unrelated definitions share the word "homogeneous": here it means the right side is zero, not that the equation is degree-one jointly in $x$ and $y$ — that's the other homogeneous ODE, solved by $v=y/x$. Reaching for the wrong method under time pressure usually starts with this exact mix-up.
