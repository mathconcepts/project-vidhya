**How JEE Main actually asks this.**

- **NAT: wave speed on a string, or in a gas, from given properties.** Direct substitution into $v=\sqrt{T_s/\mu}$ or $v=\sqrt{\gamma P/\rho}$ — no derivation required, just correct units.

- **MCQ: Doppler effect with a moving source, a moving observer, or both.** Fix the sign for each mover separately (source term in the denominator, observer term in the numerator) before combining them — a stem with both moving is really two sign decisions, not one.

- **Trap: pipe closed at one end versus open at both ends.** The phrase "closed organ pipe" or "closed at one end" restricts the answer to odd harmonics only; missing this word changes which frequencies are even possible.

- **Trap: particle velocity asked for, wave speed given (or the reverse).** Read the question twice — "speed of the wave" and "velocity of the particle at $x,t$" are different formulas with different answers.

- **Time budget:** a beat-frequency or wave-speed NAT should take under $20$ seconds — a single formula, a single substitution.
