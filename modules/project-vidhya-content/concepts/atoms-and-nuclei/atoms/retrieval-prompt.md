**Question:** State the hydrogen energy-level formula, the radioactive decay law, and the half-life relation. What does each symbol mean?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Hydrogen energy levels:** $E_n=-\dfrac{13.6}{n^2}$ eV, $n=1,2,3,\dots$ — the electron's fixed allowed energy at level $n$.

**Radioactive decay law:** $N=N_0e^{-\lambda t}$.

- $N_0$ — number of undecayed nuclei at $t=0$.
- $N$ — number remaining at time $t$.
- $\lambda$ — decay constant, fixed for a given radioactive substance.

**Half-life:** $T_{1/2}=\dfrac{\ln 2}{\lambda}\approx\dfrac{0.693}{\lambda}$ — the time for exactly half of whatever is left to decay.

</details>
