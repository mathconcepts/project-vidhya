Find the derivative of $f(x) = \sqrt{5x + 2}$.

- **(A)** $\frac{1}{2\sqrt{5x+2}}$
- **(B)** $\frac{5}{\sqrt{5x+2}}$
- **(C)** $\frac{5}{2\sqrt{5x+2}}$
- **(D)** $\frac{1}{2\sqrt{5}}$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**. Rewrite as $f(x) = (5x+2)^{1/2}$ and use the chain rule.

$$f'(x) = \frac{1}{2}(5x+2)^{-1/2} \cdot 5 = \frac{5}{2\sqrt{5x+2}}$$

</details>
