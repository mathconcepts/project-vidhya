You already did this exact argument for circles: two touch points satisfying the same equation means that equation is their connecting line.

Same move here — only the tangent formula changes per curve:

Parabola $y^2=4ax$: chord of contact from $(x_1,y_1)$ is $yy_1=2a(x+x_1)$.

Ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$: it is $\dfrac{xx_1}{a^2}+\dfrac{yy_1}{b^2}=1$.

Hyperbola $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$: it is $\dfrac{xx_1}{a^2}-\dfrac{yy_1}{b^2}=1$.

The rule that produces each one from its curve's equation: replace $x^2$ with $xx_1$, $y^2$ with $yy_1$, $x$ with $\frac{x+x_1}{2}$, $y$ with $\frac{y+y_1}{2}$.
