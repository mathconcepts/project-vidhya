Average speed over equal distances at $60$ and $40$ km/h is the harmonic mean, $48$, not the arithmetic mean, $50$. The condition that matters is EQUAL DISTANCE, not equal time — swap the setup and the answer changes completely. If the car instead drives for equal TIME at $60$ and equal TIME at $40$ (say, one hour each), the average speed genuinely is the arithmetic mean: total distance $60+40=100$ km over $2$ hours, giving $50$ km/h. Same two speeds, same two numbers, and the correct mean flips entirely depending on whether distance or time is held fixed — reading the setup, not just spotting "two speeds, find the average," is what decides which mean applies.
