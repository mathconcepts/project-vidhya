**Question:** Name the three electronic effects covered in this concept, and state in one line each how each one moves electron density.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- **Inductive effect**: a permanent pull or push of electrons through ordinary sigma (single) bonds, fading fast with distance.
- **Resonance (mesomeric) effect**: electron density moving through a connected pi system or an adjacent lone pair — never through sigma bonds.
- **Hyperconjugation**: a neighbouring carbon-hydrogen sigma bond's electron pair overlapping sideways into an empty or partly-empty orbital right next to it.

</details>
