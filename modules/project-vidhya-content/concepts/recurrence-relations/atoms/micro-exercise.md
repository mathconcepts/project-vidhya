For $a_n=3a_{n-1}$ with $a_0=4$, find $a_3$ two ways: iterating, and via the closed form.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$a_n=4\cdot3^n$, so $a_3=4\cdot27=108$. Iterating: $a_1=12,a_2=36,a_3=108$ — matches.
</details>
