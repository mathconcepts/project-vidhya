Take $x^2+y^2=25$. Differentiate both sides: $2x+2y\frac{dy}{dx}=0$. Solve: $\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{x}{y}$. At the point $(3,4)$: $\frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac34$ — found without ever isolating $y$ first.
