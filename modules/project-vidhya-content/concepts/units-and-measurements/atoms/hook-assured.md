"More decimal places means a more precise answer" is false the moment the extra digits come from arithmetic, not from a finer instrument. $a=2.20$ cm cubed gives $10.648000000000003$ cm$^3$ on a calculator — but $a$'s own precision is fixed by the caliper's least count, and no amount of multiplying can push a measurement past the resolution of the tool that produced it.

Counterexample worth remembering: two students measure the *same* cube with the *same* caliper and both report $V=10.648$ cm$^3$ — identical digits, yet they are not equally justified. One rounded honestly to the caliper's true resolution; the other simply copied every digit the calculator displayed. Matching numbers do not mean matching rigour.
