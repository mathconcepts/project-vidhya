**Problem:** Nylon-6,6 comes from hexamethylenediamine and adipic acid. Addition or condensation?

---

**Step 1 — Look for a double bond in the monomers.** Hexamethylenediamine and adipic acid: neither has one. Instead, each carries a reactive end group: $\text{-NH}_2$ on one, $\text{-COOH}$ on the other.

---

**Step 2 — Track what's released when those two ends react.** They form an amide bond, $\text{-CO-NH-}$, and release one water molecule every single time.

$$\boxed{\text{Condensation polymerisation — one water molecule per amide bond.}}$$

**Check against ethylene.** Ethylene has a double bond, and it opens with nothing released at all — that's addition, a genuinely different mechanism. Nylon-6,6 releasing water at every bond confirms it belongs in the condensation category instead.
