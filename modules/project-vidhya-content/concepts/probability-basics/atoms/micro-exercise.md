$A$ and $B$ are independent, $P(A)=0.4$, $P(B)=0.5$. Find $P(A\cup B)$.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)=0.2$ (independence). $P(A\cup B)=0.4+0.5-0.2=0.7$.
</details>
