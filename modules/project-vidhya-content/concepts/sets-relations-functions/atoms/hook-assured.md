A PNR checker returning one status per PNR is the mechanical check. Here is where JEE actually tests it: $y^2 = x$ is **not** a function of $x$, even though it looks like a fine equation. Solve for $y$ at $x=4$ and you get $y=2$ **and** $y=-2$ — one input, two outputs, so the rule fails.

Split it into $y=\sqrt{x}$ and $y=-\sqrt{x}$ separately, and each half becomes a genuine function on its own. This is the trap JEE sets: an equation relating $x$ and $y$ is only sometimes a function of $x$ — check the "one output" rule before assuming it, never after.
