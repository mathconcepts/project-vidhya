**How JEE Main actually asks this.**

- **NAT: image distance or magnification from mirror/lens data.** Assign signs first, from the sign convention, before substituting anything — decide which formula (mirror or lens) applies, then which sign each given quantity gets.

- **MCQ: identify real/virtual and upright/inverted from a calculation.** Once $v$ and $m$ come out numerically, read their signs using the correct rule for the formula in play — a lens's negative $v$ means virtual; a mirror's negative $v$ means real, the opposite reading.

- **Trap: TIR direction.** A question describing light entering a denser medium and asking about total internal reflection is testing whether the direction is remembered correctly — TIR is impossible in that direction, at any angle, and any option claiming otherwise is wrong by definition.

- **Trap: prism deviation at minimum deviation.** The condition $i=e$ (equal angles of incidence and emergence) is what defines minimum deviation — a question stating this condition is quietly telling you $A=r_1+r_2$ splits into two *equal* halves, $r_1=r_2=A/2$, without saying so explicitly.

- **Time budget:** a direct mirror or lens formula NAT should take under $45$ seconds; a "read the sign" MCQ, once $v$ and $m$ are known, should take under $15$ seconds.
