Push sixteen arrows through $A=\begin{pmatrix}4&1\\2&3\end{pmatrix}$ and two of them refuse to turn — one stretched ×5, the other ×2. But look closely: those two rails are NOT at a right angle, the way they were for a symmetric matrix. Diagonalizable only needs the rails independent, not perpendicular — in eigen coordinates $A$ is nothing more than the pair of numbers 5 and 2.
