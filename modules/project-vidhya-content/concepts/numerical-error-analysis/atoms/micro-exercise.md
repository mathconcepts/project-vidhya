$\pi=3.14159\ldots$ is approximated as $3.14$. What is the percentage error, to 2 decimal places?

- **(A)** $0.05\%$
- **(B)** $0.16\%$
- **(C)** $1.59\%$
- **(D)** $5.06\%$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. $E_a=|3.14159-3.14|=0.00159$. $E_r=0.00159/3.14159\approx0.000506$. $E_p\approx0.05\%$.

</details>
