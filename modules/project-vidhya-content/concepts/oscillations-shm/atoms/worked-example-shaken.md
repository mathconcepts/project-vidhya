**Problem:** Same block: $m=2$ kg, $k=800$ N/m, $A=0.1$ m. Find the time period, the maximum speed, and the speed at $x=0.05$ m.

---

**Draw it first.** The block slides between $-0.1$ m and $+0.1$ m, fastest at the middle, momentarily still at both ends.

---

**Find $\omega$.** $k/m=800/2=400$. Square root: $\omega=20$ rad/s.

---

**Find $T$.** $2\pi/20\approx0.314$ s.

---

**Find $v_{max}$.** Multiply amplitude by $\omega$: $(0.1)(20)=2$ m/s.

---

**Find the speed at $x=0.05$ m.** $A^2-x^2=(0.1)^2-(0.05)^2=0.01-0.0025=0.0075$. Square root: $\sqrt{0.0075}\approx0.0866$. Multiply by $\omega$: $(20)(0.0866)\approx1.73$ m/s.

---

**Confirm it is less than $v_{max}$.** $1.73<2$ — correct, since speed only reaches its peak of $2$ m/s exactly at the centre, and the block here is already partway toward an end.
