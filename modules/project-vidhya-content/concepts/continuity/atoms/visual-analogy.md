Trace $f(x)=\dfrac1x$ across a window that includes $x=0$. Coming from the left, the curve doesn't approach any finite height at all — it plunges toward $-\infty$, faster the closer $x$ gets to $0$. Coming from the right, it rockets toward $+\infty$ instead. Neither side settles anywhere, and the two sides don't even agree on a *direction*, let alone a value. This is the shape a "hole" is never a fair description of: there is no missing point to patch, because there is no finite height either side is heading toward. An **infinite discontinuity** looks nothing like a removable one, and no redefinition of $f(0)$ could ever repair it.
