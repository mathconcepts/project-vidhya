**Question:** For a projectile launched from the ground at speed $u$ and angle $\theta$, write the formulas for the horizontal and vertical velocity components, the time of flight, the maximum height, and the range.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$u_x=u\cos\theta$, $u_y=u\sin\theta$.

$$T=\dfrac{2u_y}{g}, \qquad H=\dfrac{u_y^2}{2g}, \qquad R=u_xT=\dfrac{u^2\sin2\theta}{g}.$$

</details>
