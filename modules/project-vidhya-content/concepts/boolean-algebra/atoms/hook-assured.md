Literal count, not term count, is what a K-map grade actually tracks: a three-term SOP can carry more literals than a five-term one if the grouping left an eliminable variable in — exactly the version a grader marks down. The second reliable place confidence backfires is treating the grid as flat instead of wraparound — the top row sits next to the bottom row, the left column next to the right, and a valid group can straddle that edge the picture doesn't show. Skipping the wraparound check is how an answer stops one group short of minimal without anyone noticing.
