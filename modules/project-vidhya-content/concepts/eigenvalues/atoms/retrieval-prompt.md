From memory, before checking: how do the eigenvalues of a matrix relate to its trace and its determinant?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The sum of the eigenvalues equals the trace; the product of the eigenvalues equals the determinant. (For eigenvalues $1, 2, 3$: $\text{tr}(A) = 6$, $\det(A) = 6$.)
</details>
