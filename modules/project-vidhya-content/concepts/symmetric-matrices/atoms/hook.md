A matrix $A$ where $A = A^T$ is a gateway to remarkable structure: **all eigenvalues are real, and eigenvectors from different eigenvalues are orthogonal**. This spectral theorem is why symmetric matrices appear everywhere in engineering—from stability analysis to quadratic forms to data covariance.
