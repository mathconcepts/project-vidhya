**Question:** Write the formula for displacement current, and state how $\vec{E}$ and $\vec{B}$ are oriented relative to each other and to the direction of travel in a plane electromagnetic wave.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Displacement current:** $I_d = \varepsilon_0\dfrac{d\Phi_E}{dt}$.

**Orientation:** $\vec{E}$, $\vec{B}$, and the direction of propagation are all mutually perpendicular (the wave is transverse), and $\vec{E}$ and $\vec{B}$ oscillate **in phase** with each other.

</details>
