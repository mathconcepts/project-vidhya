Circle $x^2+y^2=a^2$. Touch points $A(x_2,y_2)$, $B(x_3,y_3)$ — unknown for now.

Tangent at $A$: $xx_2+yy_2=a^2$. This line passes through outside point $P(x_1,y_1)$, so: $x_1x_2+y_1y_2=a^2$.

Tangent at $B$: $xx_3+yy_3=a^2$. This also passes through $P$: $x_1x_3+y_1y_3=a^2$.

Both boxed equations have the SAME shape — just $(x_2,y_2)$ swapped for $(x_3,y_3)$. So both $A$ and $B$ satisfy: $x_1x+y_1y=a^2$.

Two points fix a line. So $x_1x+y_1y=a^2$ is exactly the line through $A$ and $B$ — the chord of contact. $A$ and $B$'s actual coordinates were never needed.
