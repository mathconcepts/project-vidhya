AP adds, GP multiplies, HP is an AP in the reciprocals — and AM $\geq$ GM $\geq$ HM for positive numbers, equality only when every value is identical. The condition JEE tests hardest here: AM-GM-HM needs every number POSITIVE, full stop. Counterexample: take $a=-2$, $b=-8$. Their arithmetic mean is $-5$. Their "geometric mean" $\sqrt{(-2)(-8)}=\sqrt{16}=4$ is a real number here purely because the product of two negatives is positive — but the inequality chain AM $\geq$ GM breaks completely: $-5$ is not $\geq 4$. The moment a negative number enters the set, the whole AM-GM-HM chain is off the table, not just weakened — checking every term's sign before invoking the inequality is not optional caution, it decides whether the tool applies at all.
