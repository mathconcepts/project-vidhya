**Cross-concept check: alternating-current → electromagnetic-waves.**

**Question 1 (an LC circuit's natural frequency):** An LC circuit has $L = 0.5\text{ H}$ and $C = 8\ \mu\text{F}$. Find its resonant angular frequency $\omega_0$.

*Answer:* $\omega_0 = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{LC}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{0.5 \times 8\times10^{-6}}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{4\times10^{-6}}} = \dfrac{1}{0.002} = 500\text{ rad/s}$.

**Question 2 (what an oscillating current does to the space around it):** A capacitor in this LC circuit is being charged and discharged every cycle, so the electric field between its plates is constantly changing. In one sentence, connect this changing field to the next concept.

*Answer:* A changing electric field between the plates acts as a **displacement current**, and Maxwell showed that such a continuously changing electric field always comes paired with a continuously changing magnetic field around it — the two regenerating each other outward through space as an **electromagnetic wave**, at the same angular frequency the LC circuit itself oscillates at.

**Why this drill exists:** resonance is usually left as "the frequency where current is maximum" and nothing more. The same oscillating circuit is also, at the exact same frequency, generating the changing fields that the next concept describes propagating as light, radio, or any other member of the electromagnetic spectrum.
