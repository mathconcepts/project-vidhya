**Question:** State the series and parallel combination formulas for capacitance, and say which one behaves like the resistor-*parallel* rule.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Series:** $\dfrac{1}{C_s}=\dfrac{1}{C_1}+\dfrac{1}{C_2}$ — smaller than either individual capacitance. This is the one that behaves like the resistor-**parallel** rule, because in both cases it is the reciprocals that add.

**Parallel:** $C_p=C_1+C_2$ — larger than either individual capacitance. This behaves like the resistor-**series** rule instead.

</details>
