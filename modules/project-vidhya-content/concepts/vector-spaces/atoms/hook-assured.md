Closure under addition and scalar multiplication is the whole content of the 8 axioms — everything else is bookkeeping that's automatic for any subset of a real vector space. The payoff is that "vector" stops meaning "arrow": polynomials, matrices, and functions all qualify the instant they close under $+$ and scalar $\cdot$, which is why the same rank-nullity and basis machinery reappears in every one of them.
