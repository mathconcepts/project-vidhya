For basis $B=\{(1,1),(1,-1)\}$, find $[x]_B$ for $x=(5,1)$ in standard coordinates.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Solve $a(1,1)+b(1,-1)=(5,1)$: $a+b=5$, $a-b=1$. Adding: $2a=6\Rightarrow a=3$; then $b=2$.

$[x]_B=(3,2)$. Check: $3(1,1)+2(1,-1)=(3,3)+(2,-2)=(5,1)$ ✓.

</details>
