**"Zero and hero swap places."** Where displacement is zero, speed is the hero (at its biggest). Where displacement is the hero (biggest, at the extremes), speed drops to zero. They trade spots, never share one.

**"Spring keeps k and m, pendulum keeps g and L."** A spring-mass system's frequency only ever involves $k$ and $m$; a pendulum's only ever involves $g$ and $L$ — mixing the two systems' letters into one formula is the single most avoidable error in this concept.

**"Bigger swing, same beat"** — for genuine SHM, amplitude can grow or shrink without changing the time period at all. The beat (the rhythm, $T$) stays fixed; only how far it swings changes.
