**"4n-plus-2 says settle, 4n says shake."** A pi electron count landing exactly on $4n+2$ (like $2, 6, 10, 14, \dots$) means the ring settles into an unusually calm, aromatic state. The same shape landing exactly on $4n$ (like $4, 8, 12, \dots$) means the ring shakes — antiaromatic, less stable than an open chain.

**Check order: "Ring, Flat, Full loop, then Count."** Before counting anything, check the ring is cyclic, is flat (planar), and has a p-orbital on every single atom around it with no gap (fully conjugated). Only once all three hold does the electron count mean anything at all.

**For the peroxide exception, remember: "only HBr flips."** Adding $\text{HCl}$ or $\text{HI}$ with peroxide present still follows ordinary Markovnikov addition — the reversed, anti-Markovnikov outcome belongs to $\text{HBr}$ alone.
