Take $v_1=(1,0)$ and $v_2=(2,0)$. Notice $v_2=2v_1$ — the second is just a stretched copy of the first. That's dependence: one vector adds nothing new. Two vectors pointing in genuinely different directions, like $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$, are independent.
