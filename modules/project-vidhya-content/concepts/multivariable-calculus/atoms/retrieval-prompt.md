Before checking: for the same $f(x,y)=3x^2y-y^3$, what is $\partial f/\partial y$ (not $x$) at $(1,2)$?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$\partial f/\partial y=3x^2-3y^2$. At $(1,2)$: $3(1)-3(4)=3-12=-9$.

</details>
