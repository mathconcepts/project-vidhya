Ten patients, ten starting doses, one shared differential equation — the family $N(t)=N_0e^{-kt}$ has exactly ONE arbitrary constant, $N_0$, so eliminating it needs exactly one differentiation: $\frac{dN}{dt}=-kN_0e^{-kt}=-kN$, with $N_0$ gone entirely. This is the general rule behind "formation of a differential equation": a family with $n$ arbitrary constants collapses to a differential equation of order $n$, found by differentiating $n$ times and algebraically eliminating every constant along the way. The forward direction (solve a DE to recover the family) and this reverse direction (start from a family, recover its DE) are the same skill run in opposite order.
