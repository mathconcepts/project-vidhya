Climbing a hill by a direct steep path or by a longer, winding two-stage path both end at the same height gain — the total height climbed does not care which route was taken, only where you started and where you ended up.

**Enthalpy** behaves the same way, because it is a **state function**. Given: carbon burning directly to carbon monoxide releases $-110.5$ kJ/mol (step 1), and carbon monoxide burning further to carbon dioxide releases $-283.0$ kJ/mol (step 2). Adding the two "climb" segments gives $-393.5$ kJ/mol — matching carbon burning directly, in one step, straight to carbon dioxide. Two different paths, one identical total.
