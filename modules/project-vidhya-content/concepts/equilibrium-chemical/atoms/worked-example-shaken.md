**Problem:** For $A(g) \rightleftharpoons B(g)$, $K_c = 4.0$ at a given temperature. At a certain instant, $[A]=2.0$ M and $[B]=3.0$ M. In which direction, if any, does the reaction proceed?

---

**Step 1 — Write $Q$'s expression, matching $K_c$'s form exactly.** $Q = \dfrac{[B]}{[A]}$.

---

**Step 2 — Substitute the given concentrations.** $Q = \dfrac{3.0}{2.0}$.

---

**Step 3 — Simplify.** $Q = 1.5$.

---

**Step 4 — Write $K_c$'s given value next to it.** $K_c = 4.0$.

---

**Step 5 — Compare the two numbers.** $1.5 < 4.0$, so $Q<K_c$.

$$\boxed{Q < K_c}$$

---

**Step 6 — Translate the comparison into a direction.** $Q<K_c$ means: not enough product yet relative to reactant. The forward reaction runs, using up $A$ and making more $B$, until $Q$ climbs up to $4.0$.

**Check:** as the reaction runs forward, $[B]$ increases and $[A]$ decreases, so $Q=[B]/[A]$ must increase — consistent with $Q$ needing to rise from $1.5$ toward $4.0$.
