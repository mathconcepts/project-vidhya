**How JEE actually asks this.**

- **NAT/MCQ: bond order from a given bonding/antibonding electron count.** Straight substitution into $\dfrac{\text{bonding}-\text{antibonding}}{2}$ — the only real risk is a subtraction or division slip, so write both counts down before doing any arithmetic.

- **MCQ: predicting shape from a given number of bond pairs and lone pairs.** When the question states both counts directly, skip drawing the full Lewis structure — go straight to the standard shape table (2 pairs $\to$ linear, 3 $\to$ trigonal planar, 4 $\to$ tetrahedral, adjusting for lone-pair count).

- **Trap: "which molecule is paramagnetic" questions target MO theory specifically.** A Lewis-structure-only approach (all electrons paired) will always answer "none of these are paramagnetic" — wrong whenever the real answer needs an MO-theory unpaired-electron count, most classically $\text{O}_2$.

- **Trap: formal charge sign errors from mis-splitting the bonding electron count.** Forgetting to halve the bonding-electron count (using the full pair instead of half) shifts the computed formal charge by a whole unit, in a way that still looks like a plausible answer.

- **Time budget:** a direct bond-order NAT should take under 30 seconds. A shape-from-electron-pairs MCQ, closer to 45–60 seconds including checking the lone-pair adjustment.
