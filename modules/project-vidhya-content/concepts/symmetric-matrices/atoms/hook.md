Most matrices hand you complex eigenvalues and eigenvectors that lean against each other at awkward angles. Impose one condition — $A = A^T$ — and both problems disappear together: every eigenvalue comes out real, and eigenvectors belonging to different eigenvalues meet at right angles. That is why stiffness matrices, covariance matrices and quadratic forms are all symmetric by construction.
