**"1, 2, 4 — the $Z$ ladder."** Simple cubic $Z=1$, bcc $Z=2$, fcc $Z=4$ — each doubles the one before it, an easy ladder to recall the whole trio from.

**"Edge, Body, Face — where atoms touch."** Simple cubic touches along the Edge; bcc touches along the Body diagonal; fcc touches along a Face diagonal. The touching line names itself after the word that comes right before "cubic" or "centred" in each structure's own name, in order.

**"Schottky Shrinks (density); Frenkel Fudges (position only)."** A Schottky defect genuinely removes mass, shrinking density; a Frenkel defect just fudges WHERE an ion sits, leaving density untouched.
