**Find the chord of contact of tangents drawn from $(7,1)$ to the circle $x^2+y^2-2x-4y-4=0$.**

Compare to $x^2+y^2+2gx+2fy+c=0$: $g=-1$, $f=-2$, $c=-4$. Centre $(1,2)$, radius $r=\sqrt{1+4+4}=3$.

$S_1=49+1-14-4-4=28$. That is greater than $0$, so $(7,1)$ sits outside — two real tangents exist.

Chord of contact, $T=0$ at $(7,1)$: $7x+y-(x+7)-2(y+1)-4=0$.

Simplify: $7x+y-x-7-2y-2-4=0 \Rightarrow 6x-y-13=0$.

Check: distance from centre $(1,2)$ to $(7,1)$ is $\sqrt{36+1}=\sqrt{37}$. Tangent length $=\sqrt{37-9}=2\sqrt7$.

**Answer: $6x-y-13=0$.**
