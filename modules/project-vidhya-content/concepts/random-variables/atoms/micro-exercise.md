A Bernoulli random variable has $P(X=1)=0.6$, $P(X=0)=0.4$. Find $E[X]$ and $\text{Var}(X)$.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$E[X]=0.6$. $\text{Var}(X)=E[X^2]-(E[X])^2 = 0.6-0.36=0.24$ (since $X^2=X$ for a 0/1 variable, $E[X^2]=E[X]=0.6$).
</details>
