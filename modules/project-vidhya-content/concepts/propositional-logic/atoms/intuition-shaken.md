Take two statements and swap their order.

$P\to Q$: "if whale, then mammal." True — every whale is a mammal.

Swap it: $Q\to P$: "if mammal, then whale." False — a dog is a mammal, not a whale.

Same two ideas, opposite order, different truth value. So $P\to Q$ and $Q\to P$ are **not** the same statement.

Check: is $\neg Q\to\neg P$ ("not a mammal, so not a whale") also true? Yes — anything that is not a mammal cannot be a whale. This one, the **contrapositive**, always matches the original.
