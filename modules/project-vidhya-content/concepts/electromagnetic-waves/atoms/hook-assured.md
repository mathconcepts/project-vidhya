The phrase "displacement current" tempts students into imagining actual charge crossing the capacitor gap. It is not a real current of moving charge at all — it is a bookkeeping term, $I_d = \varepsilon_0\,d\Phi_E/dt$, equal in size to the real conduction current in this circuit, but made entirely of a *changing electric field* with nothing physically displaced.

What this phrase hides: without this term, Ampere's law gives a different answer depending on which surface you choose to bound the same loop — a genuine inconsistency, not a rounding gap. Displacement current is the exact fix that makes the answer surface-independent again, and it is that same fix that predicts electromagnetic waves can exist at all.
