A shop's daily profit is $P(x) = -2x^2+40x-150$ rupees, $x$ being how much price is raised above cost. At $x=5$: $P(5) = -2(25)+200-150 = 0$. Exactly zero — not a loss, not a gain yet.

At $x=10$: $P(10)=-200+400-150=50$. Positive. At $x=1$: $P(1)=-2+40-150=-112$. Negative.

So the sign flips somewhere before $x=5$ and again somewhere after it. Those flip points are the roots of $-2x^2+40x-150=0$ — a **quadratic equation**. The **discriminant** is the one number that says whether such flip points exist at all, before solving for them.
