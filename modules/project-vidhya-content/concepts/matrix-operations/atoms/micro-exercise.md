If $A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, find the $(1,2)$ entry of $AB$.

- **(A)** 1
- **(B)** 2
- **(C)** 4
- **(D)** 0

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. Row 1 of $A$ against column 2 of $B$: $(AB)_{12} = 2(0) + 1(1) = 1$.

Full product as a check: $AB = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1 \\ 6 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ — the $(1,1)$ entry is $4$ (option C), a plausible slip if you grab the wrong entry.

</details>
