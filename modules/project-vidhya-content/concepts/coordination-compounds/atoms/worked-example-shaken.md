**Problem:** Write the IUPAC name of $[Co(NH_3)_5Cl]Cl_2$.

---

**Step 1 — Split the formula into two parts.** Inside the brackets: $Co(NH_3)_5Cl$. Outside the brackets: $2$ chloride ions, $Cl^-$ and $Cl^-$.

---

**Step 2 — Add up the outside charge.** $2$ ions, each $-1$: $2 \times (-1) = -2$.

---

**Step 3 — Balance it.** The whole compound has $0$ charge overall, so the inside complex must be $+2$ to cancel the $-2$ outside: $[Co(NH_3)_5Cl]^{2+}$.

---

**Step 4 — Total the charges already present, apart from cobalt.** $5$ ammonia molecules, each $0$ charge: $5 \times 0 = 0$. $1$ chloride ligand: $-1$. Total so far: $0 + (-1) = -1$.

---

**Step 5 — Find cobalt's charge by subtraction.** Cobalt's charge plus $-1$ must equal $+2$, so cobalt's charge is $+2 - (-1) = +3$.

---

**Step 6 — Name the ligands.** $NH_3$ as a ligand is "ammine"; $5$ of them is "pentaammine". $Cl^-$ as a ligand is "chlorido"; $1$ of them stays "chlorido". "Ammine" comes before "chlorido" alphabetically, so it is written first.

---

**Step 7 — Put it together.** $\boxed{\text{pentaamminechloridocobalt(III) chloride}}$.
