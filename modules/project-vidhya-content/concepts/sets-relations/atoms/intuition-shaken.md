Take the relation "is a friend of."

Reflexive: skip it. Symmetric: usually yes — friend of $b$ means $b$ is usually a friend back. Transitive: check it. $a$ is a friend of $b$. $b$ is a friend of $c$. Is $a$ a friend of $c$? Not always — two of your friends might be strangers to each other.

So "is a friend of" fails transitivity. It cannot sort people into clean, non-overlapping groups the way "same remainder mod $3$" sorts numbers.

Now check divisibility, $a\mid b$: reflexive, transitive, but not symmetric ($2\mid4$ does not give $4\mid2$). That's a **partial order** — a different clean structure from a different pair of axioms.
