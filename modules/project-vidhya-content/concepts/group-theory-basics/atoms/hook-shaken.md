Rotate a square by $90°$ once: label the result $r$. Rotate again: $180°$, call it $r^2$. A third time: $270°$, $r^3$. A fourth: back to the start, $r^4=e$. Four rotations, and the fourth always returns you to where the first began. What property of the square makes that guaranteed, not coincidental?
