An elevator's position over time never jumps — it cannot vanish from floor $3$ and reappear on floor $5$ without passing through floor $4$. That is continuity: no holes, no jumps in the graph. Now the elevator hits the bottom, stops completely, and instantly reverses. At that exact instant, the position graph has a sharp corner — like the letter V. Check: is the position still continuous there? Yes — no jump. Is there still one clear speed at that instant? Look at the corner: the graph approaches from one direction, then leaves in a completely different direction. That mismatch is the separate question this section answers.
