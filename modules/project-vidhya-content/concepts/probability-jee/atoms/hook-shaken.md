Three machines. Machine A makes $25\%$ of all items. Its defect rate is $5\%$ — the highest of the three. Machine B makes $35\%$, defect rate $4\%$. Machine C makes $40\%$, defect rate $2\%$ — the lowest. One item is picked at random and it is defective. Which machine most likely made it? It is not automatically Machine A. A high defect rate and a small share of total output can still add up to less than a lower rate on a bigger share. Working out exactly which machine is most likely needs conditional probability, not a guess based on the defect rate alone.
