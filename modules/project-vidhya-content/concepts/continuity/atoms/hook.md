$f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-4}{x-2}$ looks like it should have a problem everywhere — division lurking in the formula. Trace it from $x=0$ toward $x=2$ and predict, before arriving: does the curve break down entirely near $x=2$, or does the trouble sit at exactly one point and nowhere else? A function can be built from a formula that "can't" be evaluated somewhere and still behave perfectly, smoothly, predictably everywhere around that one spot. Continuity is the question of whether the pen ever has to lift off the page — and the answer can hinge on a single point out of infinitely many.
