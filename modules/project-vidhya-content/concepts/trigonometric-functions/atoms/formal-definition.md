**General solution of $\sin\theta=\sin\alpha$**: $\theta=n\pi+(-1)^n\alpha$, for any integer $n$.

**General solution of $\cos\theta=\cos\alpha$**: $\theta=2n\pi\pm\alpha$, for any integer $n$.

**General solution of $\tan\theta=\tan\alpha$**: $\theta=n\pi+\alpha$, for any integer $n$.

**Compound angle formulae**: $\sin(A\pm B)=\sin A\cos B\pm\cos A\sin B$; $\cos(A\pm B)=\cos A\cos B\mp\sin A\sin B$; $\tan(A\pm B)=\dfrac{\tan A\pm\tan B}{1\mp\tan A\tan B}$.

**Transformation formulae (sum to product)**: $\sin C+\sin D=2\sin\left(\dfrac{C+D}{2}\right)\cos\left(\dfrac{C-D}{2}\right)$, and the three sibling identities for $\sin C-\sin D$, $\cos C+\cos D$, $\cos C-\cos D$ follow the same pattern with cosine and sine swapped in the right places.

**Transformation formulae (product to sum)**: $2\sin A\cos B=\sin(A+B)+\sin(A-B)$, and its three siblings for $2\cos A\sin B$, $2\cos A\cos B$, $2\sin A\sin B$.

**Sine rule**: in any triangle with sides $a,b,c$ opposite angles $A,B,C$, $\dfrac{a}{\sin A}=\dfrac{b}{\sin B}=\dfrac{c}{\sin C}=2R$, where $R$ is the triangle's circumradius.

**Cosine rule**: $\cos A=\dfrac{b^2+c^2-a^2}{2bc}$, and the two companion formulae for $\cos B$ and $\cos C$ obtained by cycling the letters.

**Projection formula**: $a=b\cos C+c\cos B$ — each side equals the sum of the other two sides' projections onto it.
