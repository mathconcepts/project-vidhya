**"Inductive relays, resonance floods, hyperconjugation borrows."** Inductive electron pull *relays* hand to hand along sigma bonds and weakens with every hand it passes through. Resonance *floods* the whole connected pi system at once, reaching just as far as the connected loop goes and no further. Hyperconjugation *borrows* a neighbouring $\text{C-H}$ bond's electron pair for a moment, without ever fully breaking that bond.

**For stability rankings, count neighbours, don't recite them.** Before naming an order, count how many carbon neighbours are actually touching the charged or radical centre. That count *is* the ranking — for a carbocation or radical, more neighbours means more stability; for a carbanion, more neighbours means *less*, because those same neighbours are now pushing extra, unwanted electron density onto an already electron-rich centre.

**Before calling any shift automatic, ask: "does the new spot beat the old one?"** A hydride or methyl group moves only toward a *more* stable destination. No comparison, no shift.
