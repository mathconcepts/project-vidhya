Inner products are the *machinery* that lets you measure angles and distances in abstract vector spaces—exactly as the dot product does in $\mathbb{R}^n$. Once you have an inner product, orthogonality, length, and projection all follow. This is the foundation for eigenvalue problems, optimization, and nearly every application of linear algebra.
