Arrows in the plane. Polynomials of degree at most three. Solutions of a linear differential equation. Continuous functions on $[0,1]$. Add any two members of any one of those lists, or scale one, and you never leave the list — which is the only property linear algebra actually requires, and the reason a single theory covers all four.
