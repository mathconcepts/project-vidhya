Heat one end of a rod. At the point $1$ m along, the temperature after $10$ minutes differs from the temperature after $30$ minutes, and both differ from the temperature at the point $2$ m along. Two numbers — where, and how long — both move the answer, so one derivative can't carry the whole story.
