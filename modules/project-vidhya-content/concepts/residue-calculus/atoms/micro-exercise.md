Find the residue of $f(z) = \dfrac{1}{(z-1)(z-2)}$ at $z=1$.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$-1$. Simple pole: $\text{Res}_{z=1}=\lim_{z\to1}(z-1)f(z)=\lim_{z\to1}\dfrac1{z-2}=\dfrac1{1-2}=-1$.
</details>
