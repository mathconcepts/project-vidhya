**"Real Men Ignore Very Unusual X-ray Ghosts."** First letters, in order of *increasing* frequency (decreasing wavelength): **R**adio, **M**icrowave, **I**nfrared, **V**isible, **U**ltraviolet, **X**-ray, **G**amma ray.

**"No charge crosses, only the field does."** A one-line reminder against imagining displacement current as real moving charge — it is the electric field itself doing the "crossing", nothing material.

**"E and B, always in step."** Unlike voltage and current in an AC circuit (which can be out of step by up to $90^\circ$), the electric and magnetic fields of an electromagnetic wave rise and fall together, in phase, every time.
