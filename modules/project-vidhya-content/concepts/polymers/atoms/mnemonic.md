**"Nothing leaves, it's addition. Something leaves, it's condensation."** Check what's released at each bond formed — that single fact, not the number of monomer types, sorts every polymer into one of the two families.

**"Cross-links are the memory of rubber."** Vulcanisation's sulfur cross-links are what let stretched rubber snap back — without them, a stretched chain simply stays stretched, remembering nothing.

**"Two different monomers doesn't mean glue was used."** Buna-S and Buna-N have two different monomers each, yet both are addition polymers — a copolymer is not automatically condensation.

**"Diamine plus diacid, water goes twice."** Nylon-6,6's amine end and acid end react and release water at every single amide bond formed along the chain — one water molecule, every time, all the way along.
