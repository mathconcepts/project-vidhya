**Question:** State Faraday's law of induction, and state in one sentence exactly what Lenz's law says the induced current opposes — the field itself, or something else?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Faraday's law:** $\varepsilon = -N\dfrac{d\Phi}{dt}$ — the induced emf equals minus the rate of change of flux linkage.

**Lenz's law:** the induced current opposes the *change* in flux that produces it — not the field's own direction. When flux is increasing, this happens to mean opposing the field; when flux is decreasing, it means reinforcing the field instead.

</details>
