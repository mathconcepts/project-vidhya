The discriminant $\Delta=B^2-4AC$ classifies a PDE by sign, exactly as it does a conic. $B$ is the actual coefficient of $u_{xy}$ in the equation as written — halving it before squaring is a different, non-standard convention some texts quietly use, and swapping the two changes elliptic/hyperbolic/parabolic outright, not by a constant factor.
