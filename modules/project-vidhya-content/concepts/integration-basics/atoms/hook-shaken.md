Drive at a steady $60$ km/h for $2$ hours. Distance $=60\times2=120$ km — the area of a $60$-by-$2$ rectangle under the speed graph. Integration is exactly this: given speed at every instant, add up the little rectangles to recover distance. Differentiation turned position into speed; integration undoes it.
