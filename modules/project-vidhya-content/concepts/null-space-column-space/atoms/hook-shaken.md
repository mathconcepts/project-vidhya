Null space: vectors $A$ sends to zero. Column space: everything $A$ can output. For $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}$, $A\begin{pmatrix}2\\-1\end{pmatrix}=0$ — that vector is in the null space.
