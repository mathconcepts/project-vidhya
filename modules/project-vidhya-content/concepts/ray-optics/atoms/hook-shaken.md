Convex lens, $f=20\text{ cm}$. Object $30\text{ cm}$ in front of it, so $u=-30\text{ cm}$ (negative, since it is measured against the incoming light).

Lens formula: $\dfrac{1}{v}-\dfrac{1}{u}=\dfrac{1}{f} \Rightarrow \dfrac{1}{v} = \dfrac{1}{20}+\dfrac{1}{-30} = \dfrac{1}{20}-\dfrac{1}{30}$.

Common denominator $60$: $\dfrac{3}{60}-\dfrac{2}{60}=\dfrac{1}{60}$. So $v=60\text{ cm}$ — positive, meaning the image forms on the far side of the lens, opposite the object: a **real** image.

Magnification: $m = v/u = 60/(-30) = -2$. Negative sign: **inverted**. Size $2$: **magnified**.

One sign choice at the very first step — $u$ negative — is what makes every later sign in the answer come out correctly.
