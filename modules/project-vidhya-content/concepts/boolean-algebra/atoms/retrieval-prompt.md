From memory: why can't a Karnaugh-map group contain exactly $3$ cells?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Valid groups must be sized as a power of $2$ ($1,2,4,8,\dots$) so the grouped variable(s) cancel out cleanly; $3$ cells always leave a variable only partially eliminated.
</details>
