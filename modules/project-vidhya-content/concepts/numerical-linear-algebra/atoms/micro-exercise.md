Perform one step of Gaussian elimination on $\begin{pmatrix}2&1&|&5\\4&3&|&11\end{pmatrix}$, using row 1 as pivot. What is the new row 2?

- **(A)** $(0,\,1\,|\,1)$
- **(B)** $(0,\,2\,|\,2)$
- **(C)** $(0,\,1\,|\,2)$
- **(D)** $(0,\,0\,|\,1)$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. $m=4/2=2$. New row 2 $=(4,3\,|\,11)-2(2,1\,|\,5)=(0,\,1\,|\,1)$.

</details>
