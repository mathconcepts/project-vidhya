Classify $(y')^2 + x\,y' - y = 0$: give its order, degree, and linearity.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Order 1, degree 2, non-linear.** The only derivative present is $y'$, so order $=1$. The equation is already polynomial in $y'$ (no roots, no trig), and the highest-order derivative — which is $y'$ itself, since it's the only one — appears to the power $2$ in the $(y')^2$ term, so degree $=2$. It's non-linear because $(y')^2$ raises the derivative to a power other than $1$.

</details>
