A car drives forward at $3$ m/s for $2$ s, then backs up at $3$ m/s for $2$ s. Area under the velocity graph: $+6$ for the forward strip, $-6$ for the reverse strip. Add them: $0$ — because the car ended up back where it started. That signed sum, not the plain area, is what the definite integral counts.
