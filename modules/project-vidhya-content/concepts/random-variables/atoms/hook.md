A vending machine dispenses 1, 2, or 3 snacks per press, with probabilities 0.2, 0.3, and 0.5. Before asking anything about "average snacks per press," first write those three probabilities down and check they sum to exactly 1 — that check is not optional, it is the only thing that makes "random variable" mean something more precise than "unpredictable number." Everything past that check — expectation, variance — is arithmetic performed on this list, not a separate idea bolted onto it.
