From memory, before checking: what are the two fixed identities linking gradient, divergence, and curl — and which one tells you a gradient field can never spin?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$\operatorname{curl}(\nabla\phi)=\mathbf 0$ (a gradient field is always irrotational — this is the one about spin) and $\operatorname{div}(\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F)=0$ (the curl of any field is always source-free).
</details>
