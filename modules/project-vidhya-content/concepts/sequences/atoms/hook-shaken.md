Take $a_n=\frac1n$: $a_1=1$, $a_2=0.5$, $a_3=0.33$. At $n=10$: $a_{10}=0.1$. At $n=10{,}000{,}000$: $a_n=0.0000001$ — closing in on $0$. The rule $a_n=1/n$ tells you every term at once; the real question is always where the terms end up, not what the rule looks like.
