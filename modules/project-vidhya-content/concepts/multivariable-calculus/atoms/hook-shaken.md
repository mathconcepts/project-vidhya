Stand on the surface $z=x^2+y^2$ at the point $(1,1)$, where $z=2$. Walk east (increase $x$, hold $y=1$ fixed): the slope is $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=2x=2$. Walk north (increase $y$, hold $x=1$ fixed): the slope is $\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=2y=2$. Both happen to match here — but walk toward $(2,1)$ instead of straight east and the story looks nothing like either single number.
