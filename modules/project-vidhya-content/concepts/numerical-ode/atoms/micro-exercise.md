Using Euler's method with $h=0.1$, approximate $y(0.1)$ for $\frac{dy}{dt}=t+y$, $y(0)=1$.

- **(A)** $y_1\approx1.1$
- **(B)** $y_1\approx1.2$
- **(C)** $y_1\approx1.15$
- **(D)** $y_1\approx1.05$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. $f(t,y)=t+y$. $y_1=y_0+h\,f(t_0,y_0)=1+0.1(0+1)=1.1$.

</details>
