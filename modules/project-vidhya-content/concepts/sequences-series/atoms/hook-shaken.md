A car drives $50$ km at $60$ km/h, then another $50$ km at $40$ km/h. Find its average speed for the whole $100$ km.

Time for the first half: $50/60$ hours. Time for the second half: $50/40$ hours. Total time: $50/60+50/40 = 5/6+5/4 = 10/12+15/12=25/12$ hours. Total distance: $100$ km. Average speed $=100 \div (25/12) = 100\times12/25=48$ km/h.

Notice this is NOT $(60+40)/2=50$. The correct value, $48$, is the harmonic mean of $60$ and $40$: $\dfrac{2\times60\times40}{60+40}=\dfrac{4800}{100}=48$. Whenever equal DISTANCES are covered at different speeds, use the harmonic mean, not the plain average.
