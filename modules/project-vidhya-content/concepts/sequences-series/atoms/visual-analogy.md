Picture the three means for $a=4$ and $b=16$ as three steps of a staircase, always going down in the same direction: AM $=10$, then GM $=8$, then HM $=6.4$. No matter which two positive numbers you start with, this staircase never runs uphill — AM is always the top step, HM is always the bottom step, and GM always sits between them.

The bars on this card are exactly those three numbers, in that order. When $a=b$, all three means collapse onto the same value and the staircase flattens into one single step — that is the one and only case where AM, GM and HM agree. The further apart $a$ and $b$ are, the steeper the staircase gets, which is why HM drags noticeably below AM in the car-speed example: $60$ and $40$ are fairly far apart, so the gap between the arithmetic mean ($50$) and the harmonic mean ($48$) already shows up, small as it looks.
