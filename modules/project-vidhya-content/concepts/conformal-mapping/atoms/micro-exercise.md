Which of $f(z)=z^2$, $f(z)=e^z$, $f(z)=\bar z$, $f(z)=|z|^2$ is conformal at every point of $\mathbb{C}$?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$f(z)=e^z$. $f'(z)=e^z\neq0$ for every $z$, and $e^z$ is entire — analytic and $f'\neq0$, everywhere. $z^2$ fails at $z=0$ ($f'(0)=0$); $\bar z$ and $|z|^2$ are not analytic at all (both fail Cauchy-Riemann off at most the origin).
</details>
