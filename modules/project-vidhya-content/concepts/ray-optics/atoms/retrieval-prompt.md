**Question:** State the mirror formula and its magnification formula, the lens formula and its magnification formula, and the one sign-convention rule that decides whether an object distance is positive or negative.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Mirror:** $\dfrac{1}{v}+\dfrac{1}{u}=\dfrac{1}{f}$, $\quad m=-\dfrac{v}{u}$.

**Lens:** $\dfrac{1}{v}-\dfrac{1}{u}=\dfrac{1}{f}$, $\quad m=\dfrac{v}{u}$.

**Sign rule:** distances measured *in* the direction of the incident light are positive; measured *against* it are negative. A real object is always measured against the incident light, so $u<0$.

</details>
