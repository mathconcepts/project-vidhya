Take a positive charge moving straight up, and a magnetic field pointing straight into the page. The angle between them is $90°$, so $\sin 90°=1$ — this is the case of maximum force.

Find the direction: curl the fingers of your right hand starting from "up" (the velocity) towards "into the page" (the field), through the smaller angle. The thumb ends up pointing to the left. So the force on this positive charge is to the left.

Now make the charge **negative** instead, keeping everything else the same. The force direction simply flips: to the right, opposite of what the right hand gave.

Now make the charge move straight up but change the field to also point straight up (parallel, not perpendicular). $\theta=0°$, $\sin 0°=0$, force is exactly zero — no direction to find at all, because there is no force.
