$L_1+\lambda L_2=0$ sweeps out every line through the intersection of $L_1=0$ and $L_2=0$ — but not quite EVERY one. As $\lambda\to\infty$, the combination approaches $L_2=0$ itself, yet no finite $\lambda$ ever lands exactly on it. $L_2$ is the one member of the family this formula can never actually write down.

That gap rarely bites, since a question almost never asks for $L_2$ back. But it matters the moment you are told "find the member of the family satisfying some condition" and the condition happens to be one only $L_2$ itself satisfies — the formula will hand you no solvable $\lambda$, and that absence of a solution is itself the answer.
