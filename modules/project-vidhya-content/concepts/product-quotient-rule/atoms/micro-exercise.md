Find the derivative of $f(x) = x \ln(x)$.

- **(A)** $\ln(x)$
- **(B)** $\ln(x) + 1$
- **(C)** $\ln(x) + x$
- **(D)** $1 + 1/x$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. Use the product rule with $u = x$ and $v = \ln(x)$.

$u' = 1$, $v' = 1/x$

$$f'(x) = 1 \cdot \ln(x) + x \cdot \frac{1}{x} = \ln(x) + 1$$

</details>
