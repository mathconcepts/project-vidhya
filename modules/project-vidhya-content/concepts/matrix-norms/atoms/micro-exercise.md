$A=\begin{pmatrix}4&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$. Find $\|A\|_2$, $\|A\|_F$, and $\kappa_2(A)$.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$A$ is diagonal, so its singular values are just $|4|,|3|$. $\|A\|_2=4$, $\|A\|_F=\sqrt{16+9}=5$, $\kappa_2(A)=4/3\approx1.33$.
</details>
