**Question:** State the first law of thermodynamics with its sign convention. Then state the Carnot efficiency formula and what each symbol means.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**First law:** $\Delta U=Q-W$, where $Q$ = heat absorbed by the gas (positive in), $W$ = work done by the gas (positive when the gas expands outward).

**Carnot efficiency:** $\eta=1-\dfrac{T_2}{T_1}$, with $T_1$ = hot-reservoir temperature and $T_2$ = cold-reservoir temperature, both in kelvin.

</details>
