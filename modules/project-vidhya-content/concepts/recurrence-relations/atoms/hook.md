A population rule: each day's count is $5$ times yesterday's count minus $6$ times the day-before's count. Day $0$: $2$. Day $1$: $5$. What's day $2$?

Plug in directly: $5(5)-6(2)=25-12=13$. One more step, day $3$: $5(13)-6(5)=65-30=35$. The numbers are climbing fast — and there's a closed formula that predicts day $n$ without repeating this arithmetic $n$ times.
