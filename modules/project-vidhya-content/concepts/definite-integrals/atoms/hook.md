A reservoir's inflow gate opens and closes with the rhythm $\sin t$ — filling through the first half of each cycle, draining through the second. After one full cycle, is the reservoir fuller than when it started, emptier, or exactly where it began? The curve clearly moves a great deal over that stretch, but movement and net change are different questions. A definite integral answers the second one: it tracks signed accumulation, where every stretch below zero subtracts instead of adding. Watch the running total below — it does something the curve's motion alone would not predict.
