Ten patients get ten different starting doses of the same drug. Patient one's level over time: $N(t)=N_0e^{-kt}$, with $N_0$ the starting dose. Differentiate: $\frac{dN}{dt}=-kN_0e^{-kt}$. Since $N_0e^{-kt}=N$, that is the same as $\frac{dN}{dt}=-kN$. The starting dose $N_0$ is gone from this last equation — it does not matter what it was. Every one of the ten patients, whatever their starting dose, satisfies this exact same equation.
