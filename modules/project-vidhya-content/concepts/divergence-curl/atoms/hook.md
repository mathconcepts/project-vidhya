Stand at one fixed point inside a flowing river and watch only what happens right there, ignoring everywhere else. Two separate questions can be asked about that single point: is fluid piling up or draining away at it — a source or a sink — and is the flow spinning around it, the way a tiny paddle wheel dropped in would turn? A field can answer yes to either question, both, or neither, entirely independently of what the same field looks like a metre away.

Before naming either operator, predict: for $\mathbf F(x,y)=(x,y)$ — flow radiating straight outward, nothing visibly circling — is there a source at the origin, a spin at the origin, both, or neither?
