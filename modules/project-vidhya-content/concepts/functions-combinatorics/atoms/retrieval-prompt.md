From memory: what's the exact relationship between $P(n,k)$ and $\binom{n}{k}$?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$P(n,k) = \binom{n}{k}\cdot k!$ — ordered selections are unordered selections times the $k!$ orderings of each chosen set.
</details>
