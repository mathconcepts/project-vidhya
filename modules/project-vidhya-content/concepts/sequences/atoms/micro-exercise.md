Which of the following sequences converges?

- **(A)** $\{n^2\}$
- **(B)** $\{(-1)^n\}$
- **(C)** $\{\frac{n+1}{2n}\}$
- **(D)** $\{e^n\}$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**. Let's check each sequence:

A) $\{n^2\}$: As $n \to \infty$, $n^2 \to \infty$. Diverges to $+\infty$.

B) $\{(-1)^n\}$: Oscillates between $-1$ and $+1$ forever. No limit exists. Diverges.

C) $\{\frac{n+1}{2n}\}$: $\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{n+1}{2n} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1 + 1/n}{2} = \frac{1}{2}$. Converges to $1/2$.

D) $\{e^n\}$: As $n \to \infty$, $e^n \to \infty$. Diverges to $+\infty$.

The correct answer is C.

</details>
