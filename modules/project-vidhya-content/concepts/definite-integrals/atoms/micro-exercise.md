Evaluate $\int_0^1 (3x^2 + 2x) \, dx$.

- **(A)** $1$
- **(B)** $2$
- **(C)** $3$
- **(D)** $4$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. Antiderivative: $F(x) = x^3 + x^2$

Apply FTC:
$$\int_0^1 (3x^2 + 2x) \, dx = F(1) - F(0) = (1 + 1) - (0) = 2$$

</details>
