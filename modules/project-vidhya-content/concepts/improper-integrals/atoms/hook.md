$\int_1^\infty \frac{dx}{x^2}$ converges to exactly 1. $\int_1^\infty \frac{dx}{x}$ runs off to infinity. Both regions are unbounded and the two integrands look nearly identical, so no amount of staring at the picture tells you which is which. You settle it by replacing the infinity with a limit and watching what the limit does.
