**Question:** Define n-type and p-type doping, and state which side of a p-n junction connects to the higher potential in forward bias.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**n-type**: doped with a pentavalent (five-valence-electron) impurity, donating one extra free electron per impurity atom. Majority carrier: electrons.

**p-type**: doped with a trivalent (three-valence-electron) impurity, creating one extra "hole" (missing electron, acting like moving positive charge) per impurity atom. Majority carrier: holes.

**Forward bias**: the **p-side** connects to the higher potential (and the n-side to the lower); this narrows the depletion region and allows large current once the barrier potential is exceeded.

</details>
