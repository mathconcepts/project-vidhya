$\tan^{-1}(1)=45°$. $\tan^{-1}(2)\approx63°$. Add them: about $108°$, a positive angle. Now try the formula $\tan^{-1}x+\tan^{-1}y=\tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{x+y}{1-xy}\right)$ with $x=1,y=2$: $\dfrac{1+2}{1-1\times2}=\dfrac{3}{-1}=-3$. So the formula gives $\tan^{-1}(-3)\approx-72°$. That is negative. Two positive angles cannot add to a negative one. Something in HOW the formula was used is wrong — not the formula itself.
