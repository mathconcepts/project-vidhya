A toll-booth boom barrier is bolted at one fixed hinge. Raise it, lower it, swing it anywhere — every position it can take is a straight line through that one hinge point. Now say you are given two of its positions as two line equations, and asked to name a third position, WITHOUT computing the hinge's coordinates.

That sounds like it needs solving two equations together. It does not. For lines $L_1=0$ and $L_2=0$, every equation $L_1+\lambda L_2=0$, for any number $\lambda$, is automatically a line through their common point — no intersection to find. Pick $\lambda$ to satisfy one extra condition, and the hinge point comes along free. This is the family of lines through a fixed point, and JEE leans on it constantly.
