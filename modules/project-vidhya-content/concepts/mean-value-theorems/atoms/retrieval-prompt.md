Does Rolle's Theorem apply to $f(x) = |x|$ on $[-1, 1]$?

- **(A)** Yes, because $f(-1) = f(1) = 1$
- **(B)** No, because $f$ is not differentiable at $x = 0$
- **(C)** Yes, and $c = 0$
- **(D)** No, because $f$ is not continuous

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. Check Rolle's conditions:
- $f$ is continuous on $[-1, 1]$ ✓
- $f(-1) = 1$ and $f(1) = 1$ ✓
- $f$ is NOT differentiable at $x = 0$ (sharp corner) ✗

Since $f$ fails the differentiability condition on the open interval $(-1, 1)$, Rolle's Theorem does not apply.

</details>
