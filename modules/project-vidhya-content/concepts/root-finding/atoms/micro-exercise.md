For $f(x)=x^2-4=0$, find $x_1$ using Newton-Raphson with $x_0=3$.

- **(A)** $x_1=2.167$
- **(B)** $x_1=2.5$
- **(C)** $x_1=2.333$
- **(D)** $x_1=2.25$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. $f'(x)=2x$. At $x_0=3$: $f(3)=5$, $f'(3)=6$. $x_1=3-\frac{5}{6}=\frac{18-5}{6}=\frac{13}{6}\approx2.1667$.

</details>
