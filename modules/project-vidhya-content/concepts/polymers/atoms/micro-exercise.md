Buna-S is made from buta-1,3-diene and styrene, both monomers carrying a carbon-carbon double bond, with nothing released when they join. Which type of polymer is Buna-S?

A) Addition homopolymer (one monomer type)
B) Addition copolymer (two monomer types)
C) Condensation homopolymer (one monomer type)
D) Condensation copolymer (two monomer types)

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct answer: B) Addition copolymer.**

Two different monomers (buta-1,3-diene and styrene) makes this a copolymer, not a homopolymer. Since both monomers join by their double bonds opening directly, with no small molecule released at any step, the mechanism is addition, not condensation.

**Why the others fail:**
- A) Wrong on monomer count — two different monomers are involved, not one.
- C) and D) Wrong on mechanism — condensation requires a small molecule (usually water) to be released at each bond formed, and nothing is released here.

</details>
