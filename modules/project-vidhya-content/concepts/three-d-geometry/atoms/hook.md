A drone is climbing in a dead-straight line, close to a tilted solar panel bolted onto a rooftop. Before it climbs any further, the pilot needs two numbers.

First: at what angle is the drone's path cutting past the panel's flat surface — steep, or almost grazing it? Second: right now, how far is the drone actually standing from that surface, measured the short way, straight across — not along its own flight path?

Both numbers come from the same two facts: the line the drone is flying, and the equation of the panel it is flying past. This concept pulls exact numbers out of exactly that kind of situation — for a line, for a plane, and for two lines that never meet at all.
