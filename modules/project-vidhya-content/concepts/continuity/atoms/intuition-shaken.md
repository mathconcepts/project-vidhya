Three curve shapes, one question each. A **hole**: both sides of the curve approach the same height, only that one point is missing or wrong — fill it in and the curve is whole again. A **step**: the left side approaches one height, the right side a different one — no single value fixes it, because the two sides disagree.

Check which shape you have by computing both one-sided limits and comparing. Equal $\Rightarrow$ hole (fixable). Different $\Rightarrow$ step (not fixable). A third shape, a **blow-up** toward infinity, is never fixable either — there is no finite height to fill in.
