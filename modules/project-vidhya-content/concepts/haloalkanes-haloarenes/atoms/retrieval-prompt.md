**Question:** State the rate law and the stereochemical outcome (inversion or racemisation) for both $S_N1$ and $S_N2$. Then state which substrate class (primary, secondary or tertiary) each mechanism favours.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- **$S_N2$**: $\text{rate} = k[\text{substrate}][\text{nucleophile}]$; gives inversion of configuration; favours a **primary** substrate.
- **$S_N1$**: $\text{rate} = k[\text{substrate}]$; gives racemisation; favours a **tertiary** substrate.

A secondary substrate can go either way, depending on the reagent and solvent.

</details>
