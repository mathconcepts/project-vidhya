**Elevation is $30°$ from a point, $60°$ from $20$ m closer. Find the tower's height.**

Step 1. Let $h$ = height, $d$ = distance from the FAR point.

Step 2. Far point: $\tan30°=h/d$, so $h=d/\sqrt3$.

Step 3. Near point, $d-20$ m away: $\tan60°=h/(d-20)$, so $h=(d-20)\sqrt3$.

Step 4. Same $h$ in both, so set them equal: $d/\sqrt3=(d-20)\sqrt3$.

Step 5. Multiply both sides by $\sqrt3$: $d=3(d-20)=3d-60$.

Step 6. Solve: $-2d=-60$, so $d=30$.

Step 7. $h=30/\sqrt3=10\sqrt3\approx17.32$ m.

Check: near distance is $10$ m. $10\tan60°=10\sqrt3$. Matches.
