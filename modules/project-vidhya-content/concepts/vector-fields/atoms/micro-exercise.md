For $\phi(x,y)=x^2y+y^3$, compute $\nabla\phi$ at $(1,2)$.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$\nabla\phi=(2xy,\ x^2+3y^2)$. At $(1,2)$: $\nabla\phi=(2\cdot1\cdot2,\ 1+3\cdot4)=(4,13)$.
</details>
