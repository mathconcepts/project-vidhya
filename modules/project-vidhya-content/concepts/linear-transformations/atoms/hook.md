A matrix is a rule that moves every arrow in the plane at once. Push a circle of arrows through $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ and watch: most tilt and stretch, but the arrow lying flat on the x-axis refuses to turn. Every arrow still starts at the origin — that point never moves. Rotating, scaling, shearing are all fair; sliding the whole picture over is not, because that would carry the origin away too.
