From memory, before checking: what exactly are the columns of the change-of-basis matrix $P_{B\to E}$?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The vectors of basis $B$, written in standard coordinates — one column per basis vector, in order. Then $[x]_E = P[x]_B$, and $[x]_B = P^{-1}[x]_E$.

</details>
