**Question:** State the formula relating moles, mass, and molar mass. Then state the difference between molarity and molality.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$n = \dfrac{\text{mass}}{\text{molar mass}}$, where $n$ is the number of moles.

**Molarity** ($M$): moles of solute per litre of solution.
**Molality** ($m$): moles of solute per kilogram of solvent.

The denominators differ — solution (solute + solvent together) versus solvent alone — so the two quantities are not interchangeable.

</details>
