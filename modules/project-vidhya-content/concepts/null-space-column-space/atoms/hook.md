Feed every vector in $\mathbb{R}^n$ through a matrix and watch where they land. Some directions get crushed to zero and never come out — that set is the null space. Everything that does come out the other side spans the column space. One records what the matrix destroys, the other what it can build, and their dimensions are forced to add up to $n$.
