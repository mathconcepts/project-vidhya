If $a,b>0$ and $a+b=10$, what is the maximum possible value of $ab$?

- **(A)** $20$
- **(B)** $24$
- **(C)** $25$
- **(D)** $30$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**C**. By AM $\geq$ GM applied to $a$ and $b$:

$$\frac{a+b}{2}\geq\sqrt{ab}\ \Rightarrow\ 5\geq\sqrt{ab}\ \Rightarrow\ ab\leq25$$

Equality in AM $\geq$ GM holds exactly when $a=b$, so the maximum $ab=25$ is achieved at $a=b=5$ (check: $5+5=10$, $5\times5=25$).

</details>
