**Question:** Write all three equations of motion for constant acceleration, and state which one quantity (of $s,u,v,a,t$) each equation leaves out.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- $v=u+at$ — leaves out $s$.
- $s=ut+\tfrac{1}{2}at^2$ — leaves out $v$.
- $v^2=u^2+2as$ — leaves out $t$.

</details>
