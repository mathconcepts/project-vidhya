# UI kit — founder portal

Operator dashboard, from `frontend/src/pages/app/FounderDashboardPage.tsx` and
`config/ceo-thresholds.json`.

- **The north-star number leads** — weekly learners who mastered a concept — with
  users, active and new as the supporting row.
- **No charts**, matching the repo's own decision to reject a 150kb charting
  library for a page glanced at once a day. Numbers are mono and tabular.
- **Absence is stated** — "No payments recorded yet" rather than an empty card.
- **Autonomy thresholds** are on the page: what agents may spend and change
  without the founder, and what always needs them.
- **Caveats are kept visible** at the end: what this view does not show.
