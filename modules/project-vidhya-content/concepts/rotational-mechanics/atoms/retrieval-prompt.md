**Question:** State the parallel axis theorem, and the rolling-without-slipping condition linking linear and angular speed.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Parallel axis theorem:** $I = I_{cm} + Md^2$, where $I_{cm}$ is about a parallel axis through the centre of mass and $d$ is the distance between the two axes.

**Rolling without slipping:** $v_{cm} = \omega R$ — the centre of mass's linear speed equals angular speed times radius, valid only when there is no sliding at the contact point.

</details>
