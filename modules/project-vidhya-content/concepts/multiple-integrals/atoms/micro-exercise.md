Evaluate $\int_0^1\int_0^1 (x+y)\,dx\,dy$.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Inner (over $x$, $y$ fixed): $\int_0^1(x+y)\,dx=\left[\tfrac{x^2}2+xy\right]_0^1=\tfrac12+y$. Outer: $\int_0^1\left(\tfrac12+y\right)dy=\tfrac12+\tfrac12=1$.

</details>
