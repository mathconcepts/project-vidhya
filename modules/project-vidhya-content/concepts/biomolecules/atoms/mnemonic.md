**"Free carbon, red mirror."** A sugar with a free anomeric carbon gives a positive Fehling's or Tollens' test (a reducing sugar). Tie both anomeric carbons up in a glycosidic bond, and there's nothing left to react — non-reducing.

**"Two for A-T, three for G-C."** Adenine pairs with thymine through two hydrogen bonds; guanine pairs with cytosine through three. More bonds, tighter grip.

**"Sequence is the recipe; fold is the dish."** Primary structure is just the amino acid order — the recipe. Tertiary structure is the actual folded three-dimensional shape it settles into — the dish. Heat can ruin the dish (denaturation) without changing the recipe at all.

**"n units, n minus one joins."** A chain of $n$ amino acids has $n-1$ peptide bonds and releases $n-1$ water molecules — the two ends of the chain are never bonded to each other.
