# Teaching Tips: Basic Derivatives

## Common Student Errors

- **Power rule sign:** Students write $\frac{d}{dx}[x^n] = x^{n-1}$ instead of $nx^{n-1}$, forgetting the coefficient.
- **Derivative of constant:** Students think $\frac{d}{dx}[5] = 5$; it's $0$.
- **Mixing up $\sin$ and $\cos$ derivatives:** $\frac{d}{dx}[\sin(x)] = \cos(x)$ (not $-\cos$), and $\frac{d}{dx}[\cos(x)] = -\sin(x)$.

## GATE Question Pattern

GATE asks: compute $f'(x)$ or $f'(a)$ for polynomials, exponentials, logarithms, or trig functions (MCQ or NAT). Often 1 mark. Combination with chain/product/quotient rules appears in 2-mark questions.

## Speed Tricks for MCQs

- **Power rule instant:** For $x^n$, just multiply by $n$ and decrease exponent by 1.
- **Exponential shortcut:** $e^x$ derivative is always $e^x$.
- **Trig memory:** SIN → COS (positive), COS → -SIN, TAN → SEC².

## Must-Memorize Formulas / Results

- **Power rule:** $\frac{d}{dx}[x^n] = nx^{n-1}$
- **Exponential:** $\frac{d}{dx}[e^x] = e^x$, $\frac{d}{dx}[a^x] = a^x \ln(a)$
- **Logarithm:** $\frac{d}{dx}[\ln(x)] = 1/x$
- **Trigonometric:** $\frac{d}{dx}[\sin(x)] = \cos(x)$, $\frac{d}{dx}[\cos(x)] = -\sin(x)$, $\frac{d}{dx}[\tan(x)] = \sec^2(x)$
