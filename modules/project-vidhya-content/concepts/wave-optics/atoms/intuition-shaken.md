$d=0.5\text{ mm}$, $D=1\text{ m}$, $\lambda=500\text{ nm}$.

Formula for path difference at height $y$: $\Delta = \dfrac{dy}{D}$.

At $y=2\text{ mm}$: $\Delta = \dfrac{(0.5\times10^{-3})(2\times10^{-3})}{1} = 1\times10^{-6}\text{ m} = 1000\text{ nm}$.

Divide by $\lambda$: $1000/500=2$. Whole number: bright.

At $y=1.5\text{ mm}$: $\Delta = \dfrac{(0.5\times10^{-3})(1.5\times10^{-3})}{1} = 750\text{ nm}$.

Divide by $\lambda$: $750/500=1.5$. Half-integer: dark.

Same formula, two heights, two opposite results — check the division by $\lambda$ every time, never assume from the height alone.
