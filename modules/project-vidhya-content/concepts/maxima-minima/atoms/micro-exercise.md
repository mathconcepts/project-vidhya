Find the critical points of $f(x) = x^3 - 3x^2 + 2$.

- **(A)** $x = 0$ and $x = 2$
- **(B)** $x = 0$ and $x = 1$
- **(C)** $x = 1$ and $x = 2$
- **(D)** $x = -1$ and $x = 2$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. Find where $f'(x) = 0$.

$$f'(x) = 3x^2 - 6x = 3x(x - 2) = 0$$

Critical points: $x = 0$ and $x = 2$.

</details>
