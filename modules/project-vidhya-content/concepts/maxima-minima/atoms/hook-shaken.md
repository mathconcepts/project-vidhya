Take $f(x)=x^2-4x+1$. Its derivative is $f'(x)=2x-4$. Set it to zero: $2x-4=0$, so $x=2$ — the one point where the tangent goes flat. Compute $f(2)=4-8+1=-3$: a valley. On a closed interval like $[0,5]$, also check the two endpoints, $f(0)=1$ and $f(5)=6$, since the smallest or largest value can sit there instead.
