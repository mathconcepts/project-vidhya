Three houses, three utilities, nine connecting lines — try to draw all nine on paper with none crossing. Every attempt leaves at least one crossing. That isn't bad luck: this graph has $6$ vertices and $9$ edges, and no arrangement in the plane avoids a crossing. Some graphs are provably flat; this one provably is not.
