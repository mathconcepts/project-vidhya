Three bars, heights $0.2$, $0.3$, $0.5$, sitting at $X=1,2,3$ — that is the entire PMF, drawn rather than listed. The bars' total height sums to exactly 1: that's the one constraint every valid PMF obeys no matter how the weight is distributed among the values. The balance point of these three bars — where the whole chart would sit level if it were a physical seesaw — is $E[X]=2.3$, pulled toward $X=3$ because that bar carries the most weight.
