Add $\dfrac12$, then $\dfrac14$, then $\dfrac18$, then $\dfrac1{16}$ — an infinite list of positive numbers, each one smaller than the last, stacked on top of each other forever. Running total after four terms: $0.9375$. Before adding a single further term, guess: does this running total blow past every ceiling, or does it lock onto one finite number and stop moving? Infinitely many positive numbers added together sounds like it should diverge on principle alone — yet here the total creeps toward $1$ and never crosses it. A series is nothing but a sequence of running totals in disguise, and predicting whether that hidden sequence settles is the entire question.
