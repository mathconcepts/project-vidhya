Push sixteen arrows through the symmetric matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&2\\2&-1\end{pmatrix}$ and watch two of them refuse to turn — the usual eigenvector story. But this time one arrow flips clean through the origin while it stretches: a negative eigenvalue, seen live, not just signed on paper. Symmetric still buys you a right angle between the two rails, no matter which way either one points.
