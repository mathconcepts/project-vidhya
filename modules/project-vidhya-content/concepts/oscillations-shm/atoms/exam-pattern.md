**How JEE Main actually asks this.**

- **NAT: speed or acceleration at a given displacement.** These reduce to plugging into $v=\omega\sqrt{A^2-x^2}$ or $a=-\omega^2x$ directly, once $\omega$ is known — no need to re-derive the full displacement equation first.

- **MCQ: identify SHM from a graph or an equation, not from the word "oscillation."** A stem may give $x(t)$ or a force law without ever saying "simple harmonic" — check whether the force (or acceleration) is proportional to $-x$ before assuming it qualifies.

- **Trap: "period is amplitude-independent" gets tested by giving a large-angle pendulum.** Beyond the small-angle range, the period genuinely does start depending on amplitude — recognising when the small-angle approximation has broken down is the actual skill.

- **Trap: spring constant units.** $k$ must be in N/m; a value quietly given in N/cm inflates every frequency and speed answer if left unconverted.

- **Time budget:** a "find speed at displacement $x$" NAT should take under $20$ seconds once $\omega$ is known — one substitution into one formula.
