Push the unit square — area exactly 1 — through the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&1.5\end{pmatrix}$ and watch its area, not its shape. It leans over into a parallelogram three times bigger. That number, 3, is the determinant. Triple the area and the determinant is 3. Flip the square inside out and the determinant turns negative. Flatten it onto a line, area zero, and the determinant is 0 — which is exactly why that matrix can never be undone.
