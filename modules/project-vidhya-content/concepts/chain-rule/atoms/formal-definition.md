**Chain Rule**: If $f = g \circ h$, meaning $f(x) = g(h(x))$, then:
$$f'(x) = g'(h(x)) \cdot h'(x)$$

Or in Leibniz notation: If $y = g(u)$ and $u = h(x)$, then:
$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy}{du} \cdot \frac{du}{dx}$$
