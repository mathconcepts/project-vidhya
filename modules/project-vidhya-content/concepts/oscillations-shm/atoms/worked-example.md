**Problem:** A block of mass $m=2$ kg, attached to a spring of stiffness $k=800$ N/m, oscillates in SHM with amplitude $A=0.1$ m. Find (a) the time period, (b) the maximum speed, and (c) the speed when displacement is $x=0.05$ m (half the amplitude).

---

**Step 1 — Picture the motion before writing anything.** The block slides on a frictionless surface between $x=-0.1$ m and $x=+0.1$ m, passing through $x=0$ at its fastest and momentarily stopping at both ends.

---

**Step 2 — Angular frequency and time period.** $\omega=\sqrt{k/m}=\sqrt{800/2}=\sqrt{400}=20$ rad/s. $T=\dfrac{2\pi}{\omega}=\dfrac{2\pi}{20}\approx0.314$ s.

---

**Step 3 — Maximum speed, at $x=0$.** $v_{max}=A\omega=(0.1)(20)=2$ m/s.

---

**Step 4 — Speed at $x=0.05$ m, using energy conservation.** $v=\omega\sqrt{A^2-x^2}=20\sqrt{(0.1)^2-(0.05)^2}=20\sqrt{0.0075}\approx1.73$ m/s.

---

**Step 5 — State all three together.** $\boxed{T\approx0.314\ \text{s},\quad v_{max}=2\ \text{m/s},\quad v(x=0.05)\approx1.73\ \text{m/s}}$. Notice $1.73$ m/s is *less* than the maximum $2$ m/s, exactly as the picture in Step 1 predicts — speed keeps falling as the block moves away from centre, reaching zero only at the very ends.
