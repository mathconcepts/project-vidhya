A **level curve** of $f(x,y)=x^2+y^2$ is a circle — every point on it shares the same height, like a contour line on a topographic map. Closer-together contours mean steeper terrain; farther-apart ones mean gentler terrain. The gradient at any point is perpendicular to the contour through it, pointing directly toward the nearest, tightest-packed neighboring contour — the steepest way up. Reading a contour map is reading a multivariable function's partial derivatives without computing a single one.
