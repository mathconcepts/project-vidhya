Calculate the divergence of $\mathbf{F}(x, y, z) = x^2\mathbf{i} + y^2\mathbf{j} + z^2\mathbf{k}$ at the point $(1, 2, 3)$.

- **(A)** 12
- **(B)** 14
- **(C)** 6
- **(D)** 8

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. The divergence is:
$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F} = \frac{\partial(x^2)}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial(y^2)}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial(z^2)}{\partial z} = 2x + 2y + 2z$$

At point $(1, 2, 3)$:
$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F}(1, 2, 3) = 2(1) + 2(2) + 2(3) = 2 + 4 + 6 = 12$$

Geometrically, the value 12 means the field is expanding outward at this point with a net source strength of 12 per unit volume.

</details>
