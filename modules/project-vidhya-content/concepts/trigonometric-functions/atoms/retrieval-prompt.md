**Recall**: What is the general solution of $\tan\theta=\tan\alpha$, and why does it need no alternating sign or $\pm$, unlike sine and cosine's formulas?

<!-- answer -->

**Answer**: $\theta=n\pi+\alpha$, for any integer $n$. Tangent already repeats every $\pi$ (half of sine and cosine's $2\pi$), because $\tan(\theta+\pi)=\tan\theta$ directly — there is only one mirror-free family of solutions per period, so a single "$+\alpha$" is enough. Sine and cosine repeat every $2\pi$ but ALSO hit the same value twice within that one period (at an angle and its mirror image), which is what forces their formulas to carry an extra correction term.
