**Problem:** A concave mirror has a focal length of magnitude $15\text{ cm}$. An object is placed $30\text{ cm}$ in front of it. Find the image distance and magnification, and state the image's nature.

---

**Step 1 — Write down the signed values.** Concave mirror: $f=-15\text{ cm}$. Object in front: $u=-30\text{ cm}$.

---

**Step 2 — Apply the mirror formula.** $\dfrac{1}{v}=\dfrac{1}{f}-\dfrac{1}{u} = \dfrac{1}{-15}-\dfrac{1}{-30}$.

---

**Step 3 — Simplify the right side.** $-\dfrac{1}{15}+\dfrac{1}{30} = -\dfrac{2}{30}+\dfrac{1}{30} = -\dfrac{1}{30}$.

---

**Step 4 — Invert to get $v$.** $v=-30\text{ cm}$.

---

**Step 5 — Compute magnification.** $m=-v/u = -(-30)/(-30) = -1$.

---

**Step 6 — Run the check.** $u=-30\text{ cm}$ is exactly $2f=-30\text{ cm}$ — the centre of curvature. An object at the centre of curvature must form its image at the same point, real, inverted, same size. $v=-30\text{ cm}$ and $m=-1$ match that exactly.

$$\boxed{v=-30\text{ cm}, \quad m=-1}$$
