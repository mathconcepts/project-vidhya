Is $P \land \neg P$ a tautology, a contradiction, or neither?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

A contradiction — for either truth value of $P$, one of $P$ and $\neg P$ is false, so the conjunction is false in both rows of the truth table.
</details>
