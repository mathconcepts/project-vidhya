Zoom in on $y=x^2$ near $x=0$ and it flattens into a line. Zoom in on $y=|x|$ near $x=0$ and the corner never goes away: slope from the left is $-1$, slope from the right is $+1$ — two different numbers, so there is no single tangent. That mismatch is exactly what "not differentiable" means, even though $|x|$ never breaks or jumps.
