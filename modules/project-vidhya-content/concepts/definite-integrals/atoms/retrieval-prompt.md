Evaluate $\int_1^2 \frac{1}{x} \, dx$.

- **(A)** $\ln(2)$
- **(B)** $\ln(1/2)$
- **(C)** $e - 1$
- **(D)** $1 - e$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. Antiderivative: $F(x) = \ln|x|$

$$\int_1^2 \frac{1}{x} \, dx = [\ln(x)]_1^2 = \ln(2) - \ln(1) = \ln(2) - 0 = \ln(2)$$

</details>
