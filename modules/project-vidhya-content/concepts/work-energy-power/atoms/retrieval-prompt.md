**Question:** State the work-energy theorem. Then state the one condition needed for mechanical energy ($KE+PE$) to be conserved.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Work-energy theorem:** $W_{net} = \Delta KE$ — the total work done by every force acting on an object equals its change in kinetic energy. This is always true.

**Mechanical energy is conserved** only when the net work done by non-conservative forces (friction, air drag, an applied push) is zero.

</details>
