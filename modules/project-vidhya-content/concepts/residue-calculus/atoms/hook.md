A contour integral asks you to accumulate a function's values all the way around a closed loop. Residue calculus says: don't. However the loop wanders, the answer depends only on one number attached to each pole trapped inside it. Sum those residues, multiply by $2\pi i$, and the integral is finished. Below, watch a loop trap two poles at once — their residues cancel to zero.
