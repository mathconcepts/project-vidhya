**Question:** State which question valence bond theory answers and which question crystal field theory answers for a coordination compound. Then state the IUPAC rule for naming an anionic complex.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**VBT answers:** the shape/geometry, via the hybridisation the metal uses ($sp^3$, $dsp^2$, $sp^3d^2$, $d^2sp^3$).

**CFT answers:** the colour and the exact magnetic behaviour, via how strongly the ligands split the $d$ orbitals into different energy levels.

**Anionic complex naming rule:** the metal name takes the $-ate$ ending, and several metals switch to their Latin root (iron $\to$ ferrate, copper $\to$ cuprate, silver $\to$ argentate).

</details>
