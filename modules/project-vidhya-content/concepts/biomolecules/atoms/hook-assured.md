"Identify the building block, identify the biomolecule" works for silk versus cotton, but do not stop at "protein" once you spot amino acids — sequence and shape matter just as much as composition. Two proteins built from an identical set of amino acids can behave completely differently if those amino acids are joined in a different order, since that order decides how the chain folds. A question naming "the same amino acid composition" is not describing "the same protein" — the sequence, not just the ingredient list, is what a real identification question is checking.
