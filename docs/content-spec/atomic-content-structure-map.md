## Base structure contract

Every topic should begin with a short attention capture, move immediately to intuition or a concrete representation, introduce the formal rule only after the learner has a mental anchor, show one worked example, expose one boundary or misconception, ask for active recall, and finish with the relevant assessment-mode or time/risk variant. The exact order can be shortened for a deadline-constrained learner, but the concept, verification and retrieval anchors should not be removed without an explicit policy reason.

## Personalization contract

Personalized content should attach to stable base anchors. It may repair a prerequisite, change representation, slow or compress pacing, add a custom-PDF clarification, add a verified computation, switch language, expose a misconception contrast, or provide an assessment-mode variant. It should not regenerate the entire lesson unless the base is missing or a high-impact conflict requires review.
