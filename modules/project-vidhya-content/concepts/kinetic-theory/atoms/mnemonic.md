**"Probable, then Plain, then Root" — P before P before R, and the letters get taller as the speeds get faster.** Most Probable speed is the smallest; Plain average speed sits in the middle; Root-mean-square speed is the largest. Same order, every gas, every temperature.

**"Three, then Three-plus-two" for degrees of freedom.** A monatomic gas only ever gets $3$ (translation). A diatomic gas gets those same $3$, *plus* $2$ more for rotation — never forget the "plus," or internal energy comes out short by a full $nRT$ per mole.

**For units: "kay-gee before you take the root."** Always convert molar mass to kilograms per mole before it goes anywhere near a square root sign — grams sneaking into $v_{rms}$, $v_{avg}$, or $v_p$ inflates the answer by about $32$ times.
