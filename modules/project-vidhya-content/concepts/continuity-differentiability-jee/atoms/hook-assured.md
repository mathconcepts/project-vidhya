An elevator that stops dead and instantly reverses is continuous in position (no floor is skipped) but has a corner in its position graph at the reversal instant — the two one-sided speeds there disagree, so no single speed exists. This is the exact structure of $f(x)=|x|$ at $x=0$: continuous everywhere, differentiable nowhere near that one point. What actually matters is the direction of the implication — differentiable always forces continuous, but continuous never forces differentiable. A JEE distractor built on "the function is continuous, so it must be differentiable" is testing exactly this asymmetry, not a computation.
