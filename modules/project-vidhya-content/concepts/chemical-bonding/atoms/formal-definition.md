**Ionic bond**: electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions formed by complete transfer of one or more electrons from one atom to another.

**Covalent bond**: a shared pair of electrons between two atoms.

**Formal charge**: $\text{FC} = V - L - \dfrac{B}{2}$, where $V$ = valence electrons of the free atom, $L$ = lone-pair (non-bonding) electrons on that atom in the structure, $B$ = bonding electrons around that atom in the structure.

**VSEPR (Valence Shell Electron Pair Repulsion)**: predicts molecular *shape* by treating every electron pair — bonding and lone — around the central atom as repelling every other pair, arranging them to minimise repulsion.

**Hybridisation**: mixing of atomic orbitals on the central atom (e.g. one $s$ and three $p$ orbitals $\to$ four $sp^3$ orbitals) into a new set of equivalent orbitals that matches the shape VSEPR predicts.

**Molecular orbital (MO) theory**: combines atomic orbitals from both bonded atoms into bonding and antibonding molecular orbitals. **Bond order** $= \dfrac{(\text{bonding electrons}) - (\text{antibonding electrons})}{2}$; predicts bond stability and unpaired-electron count (magnetism).

**Bond parameters**: bond length, bond angle, bond enthalpy (energy needed to break one mole of the bond).

**Hydrogen bonding**: a strong dipole–dipole attraction between a hydrogen atom bonded to a highly electronegative atom (N, O, F) and a lone pair on a nearby electronegative atom.
