Water flowing downhill on its own is like a **galvanic cell**: the reaction runs by itself, releasing energy, exactly the way water releases energy as it falls. A water wheel placed in that downhill flow can capture some of that energy as useful work — just as a galvanic cell's downhill "flow" of electrons can be tapped as electric current.

Forcing that same water back UPHILL needs a pump — energy has to be put in, since the water will not climb on its own. An **electrolytic cell** is exactly this pump: an external battery forces electrons to flow in the direction they would never go by themselves, driving a reaction that could never happen on its own. The chemistry inside both cells can even be identical, run in opposite directions — only whether energy is being released or force-fed decides which kind of cell it is.
