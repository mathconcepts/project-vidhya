Push a crate due north — how much of that push moved it east? None, and that's what orthogonal means, made operational. Push sixteen arrows through a $40°$ rotation and watch: every arrow turns by the same angle, yet not one of them grows or shrinks — the circle of tips stays a perfect circle, same size, the whole time. Lengths and the angles between arrows survive intact; only each arrow's own direction changes.
