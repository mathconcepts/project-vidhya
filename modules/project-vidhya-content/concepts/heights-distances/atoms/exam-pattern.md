- **NAT**: height and distance answers on JEE are usually left in surd form (like $10\sqrt3$), not as a rounded decimal — do not convert to a decimal unless the question explicitly asks for one.
- **Trap**: a two-vertical-planes question hides the fact that the two given directions are PERPENDICULAR. If the problem does not say so directly, look for "due east" and "due north," or a rectangular field, to confirm the right angle before reaching for $c^2=p^2+q^2$.
- **Time**: a one-vertical-plane problem with two positions and two angles reduces to two simultaneous equations sharing one unknown height — solve for that shared height first, not the distance, since the height is what both equations agree on.
- **MCQ**: options are usually built from the same surd family as the correct answer (for example $h$, $h/\sqrt3$, $h\sqrt3$, $2h$). If your answer does not match any surd-family option, recheck which side of the triangle you called "opposite."
- **Trap**: when a problem gives the length of a ladder or string instead of a horizontal distance, using $\tan\theta=h/d$ anyway (rather than $\sin\theta=h/\ell$) is a common, silent wrong-ratio error.
