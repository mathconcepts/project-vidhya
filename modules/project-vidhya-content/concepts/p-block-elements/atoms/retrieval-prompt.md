**Question:** State the inert pair effect and name the two elements where it is most obvious. Then state the general formula for an interhalogen and which atom is always central.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Inert pair effect:** the outermost $s$-electron pair of a heavy $p$-block atom resists forming new bonds (poor shielding by inner $d$/$f$ electrons, poor orbital overlap). Most obvious in **thallium** ($Tl^+$ more stable than $Tl^{3+}$) and **lead** ($Pb^{2+}$ more stable than $Pb^{4+}$).

**Interhalogen general formula:** $XX'_n$, where $n=1,3,5,7$. **Central atom:** always $X$, the larger and less electronegative of the two halogens.

</details>
