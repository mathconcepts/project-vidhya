For $f(x,y)=3x^2y-y^3$, find $\partial f/\partial x$ at $(1,2)$.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$\partial f/\partial x=6xy$. At $(1,2)$: $6(1)(2)=12$.

</details>
