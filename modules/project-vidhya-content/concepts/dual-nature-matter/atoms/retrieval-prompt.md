**Question:** State Einstein's photoelectric equation and the de Broglie wavelength relation. What does each symbol stand for?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Einstein's photoelectric equation:** $KE_{max}=hf-\phi$, valid only for $f\ge f_0$ (the threshold frequency).

- $h$ — Planck's constant.
- $f$ — frequency of the incoming light.
- $\phi$ — work function, the least energy needed to free one electron from the surface.
- $KE_{max}$ — maximum kinetic energy of an emitted photoelectron.

**De Broglie wavelength:** $\lambda=\dfrac{h}{p}$, where $p=mv$ is the moving particle's momentum (mass times velocity).

</details>
