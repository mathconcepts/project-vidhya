**Question:** State the reaction quotient $Q$'s relation to $K$, and what each of the three cases ($Q<K$, $Q>K$, $Q=K$) means. Then state, in one line, what a catalyst does and does not change at equilibrium.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$Q$ uses the same expression as $K$, evaluated at any point.

- $Q<K$: reaction shifts forward (toward products).
- $Q>K$: reaction shifts in reverse (toward reactants).
- $Q=K$: system is at equilibrium.

**Catalyst:** speeds up reaching equilibrium (both directions equally); never changes $K$'s value or the equilibrium position.

</details>
