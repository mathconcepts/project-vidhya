From memory, before checking: for $A = \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, find the $(2,1)$ entry of $A^{-1}$.

- **(A)** 2
- **(B)** $-2$
- **(C)** 1
- **(D)** $-1$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. $\det(A) = 3(1)-1(2) = 1$. $A^{-1} = \frac{1}{1}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ -2 & 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ -2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$.

The $(2,1)$ entry is $-2$.

</details>
