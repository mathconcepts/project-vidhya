Your eye level is a horizontal line. Look up at something above it: the angle between the horizontal and your line of sight is the angle of elevation. Look down at something below it: the same kind of angle is the angle of depression. Same idea, two names.

One useful fact: from the top of a tower, looking down at a point on the ground, the angle of depression equals the angle of elevation that point would measure looking up. The two horizontals are parallel, so the angles match.

Why tangent: these problems give you two legs of a right triangle — height and horizontal distance — never the slanted side directly. Tangent is built from exactly those two legs, so it is the ratio to reach for first.
