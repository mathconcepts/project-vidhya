**Question:** State Newton's three laws in your own words. Then state the formula for the acceleration of a system with mass $m_1$ on a frictionless table connected over a pulley to a hanging mass $m_2$.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- **First law:** an object keeps its current velocity unless a net force acts on it.
- **Second law:** $F_{\text{net}}=ma$, in the direction of the net force.
- **Third law:** every action force has an equal, opposite reaction force acting on a *different* object.

**Connected system:** $a=\dfrac{m_2g}{m_1+m_2}$.

</details>
