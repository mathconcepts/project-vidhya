**Solve $2\sin^2\theta-\sin\theta-1=0$.**

Step 1. Let $s=\sin\theta$. The equation becomes $2s^2-s-1=0$ — an ordinary quadratic.

Step 2. Factor: $2s^2-s-1=(2s+1)(s-1)$. Check by expanding: $2s^2-2s+s-1=2s^2-s-1$. Correct.

Step 3. So $2s+1=0$ gives $s=-1/2$, or $s-1=0$ gives $s=1$.

Step 4. $\sin\theta=1$: this is one special value. General solution: $\theta=2n\pi+\pi/2$.

Step 5. $\sin\theta=-1/2$: write $-1/2=\sin(-\pi/6)$, since $\sin(-\pi/6)=-1/2$. Apply $\theta=n\pi+(-1)^n\alpha$ with $\alpha=-\pi/6$: $\theta=n\pi+(-1)^{n+1}\pi/6$.

Step 6. Check $n=0$: $\theta=-\pi/6$. $\sin(-\pi/6)=-1/2$. Matches.

**Answer**: $\theta=2n\pi+\pi/2$ or $\theta=n\pi+(-1)^{n+1}\pi/6$.
