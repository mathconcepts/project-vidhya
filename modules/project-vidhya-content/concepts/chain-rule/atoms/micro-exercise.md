Find the derivative of $f(x) = \sin(3x)$.

- **(A)** $\cos(3x)$
- **(B)** $3\cos(3x)$
- **(C)** $3\sin(3x)$
- **(D)** $\cos(x)$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. Use the chain rule. Let $u = 3x$, so $f(x) = \sin(u)$.

$$f'(x) = \cos(u) \cdot \frac{du}{dx} = \cos(3x) \cdot 3 = 3\cos(3x)$$

</details>
