Which is NOT a property of the least squares solution $\hat x$ to $Ax=b$ ($A$ is $m\times n$, $m>n$)?

(A) $\hat x$ minimizes $\|b-Ax\|^2$ (B) $A^TA\hat x=A^Tb$ (C) $A\hat x=b$ exactly (D) $b-A\hat x \perp \text{col}(A)$

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**(C).** For a genuine overdetermined system, no $x$ satisfies $Ax=b$ exactly — that's the premise. (A), (B), (D) all hold.
</details>
