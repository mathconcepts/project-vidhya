$f(x)=|x|$ never breaks — trace it anywhere and the pen never lifts. But watch the *slope* as the trace crosses $x=0$: predict, before it happens, whether the steepness turns smoothly through that point or snaps instantly from one value to another. A continuous curve promises no gaps. It makes no promise at all about the rate of change staying steady — and the gap between those two promises is exactly what differentiability adds on top of continuity.
