**Resolution of a vector**: a vector of magnitude $u$ at angle $\theta$ from the positive $x$-axis has components $u_x=u\cos\theta$ (horizontal) and $u_y=u\sin\theta$ (vertical).

**Projectile launched from the ground at angle $\theta$ with speed $u$** (upward positive, launch point as origin, $g$ pointing down):
$$u_x = u\cos\theta,\qquad u_y=u\sin\theta.$$
$$\text{Time of flight: } T=\dfrac{2u_y}{g}, \qquad \text{Maximum height: } H=\dfrac{u_y^2}{2g}, \qquad \text{Range: } R=u_xT=\dfrac{u^2\sin2\theta}{g}.$$

**Trajectory equation** (eliminating $t$ between $x=u_xt$ and $y=u_yt-\tfrac{1}{2}gt^2$): $y = x\tan\theta - \dfrac{gx^2}{2u^2\cos^2\theta}$ — a parabola in $x$.

**Uniform circular motion**: an object moving at constant speed $v$ around a circle of radius $r$ has **centripetal acceleration** $a_c=\dfrac{v^2}{r}$, directed from the object toward the centre. **Angular velocity**: $\omega=v/r$, and $v=\omega r$.

**Relative velocity (2D)**: for objects $A$ and $B$ with velocities $\vec{v}_A$ and $\vec{v}_B$ in the same frame, the velocity of $A$ relative to $B$ is $\vec{v}_{AB}=\vec{v}_A-\vec{v}_B$, applied separately to $x$- and $y$-components.
