Are the vectors $(1, 2, 2)$ and $(2, 1, -2)$ orthogonal?

- **(A)** Yes, their dot product is 0
- **(B)** No, their dot product is positive
- **(C)** No, their dot product is negative
- **(D)** Cannot determine

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. Compute the dot product:
$(1, 2, 2) \cdot (2, 1, -2) = 1(2) + 2(1) + 2(-2) = 2 + 2 - 4 = 0$.

Since the dot product is 0, the vectors are orthogonal.

</details>
