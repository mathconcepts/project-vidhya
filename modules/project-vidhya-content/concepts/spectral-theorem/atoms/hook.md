Every symmetric matrix hides a secret: it can be perfectly decomposed into orthogonal directions where it acts like pure scaling. This is the Spectral Theorem — and it unlocks matrix functions like $\sqrt{A}$, $A^{100}$, and $e^A$ that would otherwise be intractable.
