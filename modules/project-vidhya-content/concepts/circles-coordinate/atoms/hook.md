A security drone hovers outside a circular restricted zone and fires two laser trip-wires that just graze the boundary, touching it at exactly one point each. Someone asks: where does the straight line joining those two grazing points sit? You would expect to first find both touch points, then work out the line through them — two separate, fiddly steps.

You don't need either step. For the circle $x^2+y^2=a^2$ and an outside point $(x_1,y_1)$, the line joining the two touch points is simply $xx_1+yy_1=a^2$ — swap one $x$ and one $y$ for the outside point's own coordinates, and the equation of that connecting line falls out directly. This is the chord of contact, and it works even when the touch points themselves are messy surds.
