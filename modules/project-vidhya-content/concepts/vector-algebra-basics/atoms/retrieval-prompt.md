From memory, before checking: are $\vec a=(3,-2,1)$ and $\vec b=(2,4,2)$ perpendicular?

- **(A)** Yes, $\vec a\cdot\vec b=0$
- **(B)** No, $\vec a\cdot\vec b=8$
- **(C)** No, $\vec a\cdot\vec b=12$
- **(D)** Cannot tell without the angle

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. $\vec a\cdot\vec b=(3)(2)+(-2)(4)+(1)(2)=6-8+2=0$. A zero dot product means perpendicular, regardless of either vector's magnitude.

</details>
