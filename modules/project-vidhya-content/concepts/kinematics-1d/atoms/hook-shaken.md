A ball is thrown straight up at $u=20$ m/s. Origin at the ground. **Upward is positive** — decide that before touching a single number. So $u=+20$ m/s, and gravity is $a=-10$ m/s$^2$, negative because it points down, opposite to our chosen positive direction, no matter which way the ball is currently moving.

Now use $v=u+at=20-10t$ and plug in three times. At $t=0$: $v=20-0=20$ m/s. At $t=2$: $v=20-20=0$ m/s — this is the top, momentarily at rest. At $t=4$: $v=20-40=-20$ m/s.

Look closely at that last number: $-20$ m/s, under our convention, means moving *downward* at $20$ m/s. It matches the launch speed exactly; only the sign flipped, marking the direction reversal.
