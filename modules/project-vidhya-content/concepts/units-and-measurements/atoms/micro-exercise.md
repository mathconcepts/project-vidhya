The length and breadth of a rectangular plate are measured as $l = 5.7$ cm and $b = 2.4$ cm. What is the area, reported to the correct number of significant figures?

A) $13.68$ cm$^2$
B) $13.7$ cm$^2$
C) $14$ cm$^2$
D) $13$ cm$^2$
E) $14.0$ cm$^2$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct answer: C) $14$ cm$^2$.**

$l \times b = 5.7 \times 2.4 = 13.68$ cm$^2$ on a calculator. Both $l$ and $b$ have exactly $2$ significant figures, so for a *multiplication*, the answer is limited to the fewest significant figures among the inputs — $2$. Rounding $13.68$ to $2$ significant figures gives $14$.

**Why the others fail:**
- A) $13.68$ keeps all four digits the calculator shows, ignoring that both inputs only carry $2$ significant figures.
- B) $13.7$ rounds to $3$ significant figures, one too many.
- D) $13$ is $2$ significant figures but rounded the wrong way — $13.68$ is closer to $14$ than to $13$.
- E) $14.0$ writes a third significant figure (the trailing zero after the decimal) that the measurement does not support.

</details>
