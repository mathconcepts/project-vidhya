**Find the chord of contact of tangents drawn from $(10,8)$ to the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$.**

Here $a^2=25$, $b^2=16$.

First confirm the point sits outside the ellipse: $S_1=\dfrac{100}{25}+\dfrac{64}{16}-1=4+4-1=7$, which is greater than zero, so $(10,8)$ lies outside.

Chord of contact: $\dfrac{xx_1}{a^2}+\dfrac{yy_1}{b^2}=1$ with $(x_1,y_1)=(10,8)$: $\dfrac{10x}{25}+\dfrac{8y}{16}=1$.

Simplify: $\dfrac{2x}{5}+\dfrac{y}{2}=1$. Multiply by $10$: $4x+5y-10=0$.

Check: the point $\left(\dfrac{5}{4}+\dfrac{5\sqrt7}{4},1-\sqrt7\right)$ lies on both the ellipse and this line.

**Answer: $4x+5y-10=0$.**
