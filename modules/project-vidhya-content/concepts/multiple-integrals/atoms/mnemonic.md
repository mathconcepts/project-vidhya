**Inside-Out, Freeze-Then-Free.** Work the INNER integral first, treating the outer variable exactly like a partial derivative treats it — frozen. Once the inner integral is done, the outer variable is FREE again, and the outer integral runs normally.

**Worked micro-example.** $\int_0^1\int_0^1 1\,dy\,dx=\int_0^1 1\,dx=1$ — the area of the unit square, the simplest possible sanity check for the whole technique.

**Sanity-check reflex.** Before integrating, ask: is my inner bound a NUMBER, or a FUNCTION of the outer variable? If the region isn't a rectangle, it has to be a function — a number there is a sign the bounds were copied from the wrong kind of region.
