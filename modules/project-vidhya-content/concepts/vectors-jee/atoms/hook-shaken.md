Picture a rope tied to a crate, pulled at an angle to the ground.

Only part of the pull moves the crate forward. The rest just presses the crate into the ground — it does nothing useful. That useful part is one number, built from the rope's direction and its strength. That number is the **dot product**.

Now tie the same rope to one corner of the crate instead of the middle. Pull hard. The crate does not slide — it swings, one edge lifting up.

That swinging effect is a second, different number, built from the same two facts about the rope. That number is the **cross product**.

Two vectors. Two different questions. Two different answers.
