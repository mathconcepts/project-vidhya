**Problem:** Same siren: $f=500$ Hz, $v_s=20$ m/s, $v=340$ m/s (sound). Find the heard frequency approaching, then receding.

---

**Fix which case is which.** Approaching: subtract $v_s$ in the denominator. Receding: add $v_s$ instead.

---

**Approaching, one number at a time.** $340-20=320$. Divide: $340/320=1.0625$. Multiply by $f$: $500\times1.0625=531.25$ Hz.

---

**Receding, one number at a time.** $340+20=360$. Divide: $340/360\approx0.9444$. Multiply by $f$: $500\times0.9444\approx472.22$ Hz.

---

**Check the direction of change makes sense.** Approaching gave $531.25>500$ (higher, correct — moving closer should raise pitch). Receding gave $472.22<500$ (lower, correct — moving away should drop pitch).
