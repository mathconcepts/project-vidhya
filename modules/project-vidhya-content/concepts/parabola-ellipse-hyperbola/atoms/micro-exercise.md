Tangents are drawn from $(10,8)$ to the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{25}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$. Find the equation of the chord of contact.

**(A)** $4x+5y-10=0$
**(B)** $4x+5y+10=0$
**(C)** $5x+4y-10=0$
**(D)** $2x+5y-10=0$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**(A).**

$a^2=25$, $b^2=16$. Chord of contact from $(x_1,y_1)$: $\dfrac{xx_1}{a^2}+\dfrac{yy_1}{b^2}=1$.

At $(10,8)$: $\dfrac{10x}{25}+\dfrac{8y}{16}=1 \Rightarrow \dfrac{2x}{5}+\dfrac{y}{2}=1$.

Multiply by $10$: $4x+5y-10=0$.

</details>
