**"An Ox, Red Cat"** — ANode is OXidation, REDuction is at the CAThode. This never changes, in a galvanic cell or an electrolytic one.

**"Galvanic: Negative Anode. Electrolytic: flip it."** A galvanic cell's anode is negative (it is the source pushing electrons out on its own); an electrolytic cell's anode is positive (an external battery is now pulling electrons out of it instead).

**"Zero-Five-Nine-One" for the Nernst constant.** $\dfrac{2.303RT}{F}$ at $298\ \text{K}$ works out to $0.0591\ \text{V}$ — worth memorising as one number rather than recomputing it from $R$, $T$, and $F$ every time.
