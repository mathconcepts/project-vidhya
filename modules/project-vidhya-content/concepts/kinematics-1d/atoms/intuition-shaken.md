Take a scooter starting at rest ($u=0$) that speeds up at a steady $a=2$ m/s$^2$ for $t=5$ s, moving in the positive direction the whole time.

Find its velocity at $t=5$: $v=u+at=0+2(5)=10$ m/s.

Find how far it went: $s=ut+\tfrac{1}{2}at^2 = 0+\tfrac{1}{2}(2)(25)=25$ m.

Check the two answers agree, using the third equation: $v^2=u^2+2as \Rightarrow 10^2=0+2(2)(25) \Rightarrow 100=100$. It matches, so both answers are consistent with each other.

On a velocity-time graph, this motion is a straight line rising from $(0,0)$ to $(5,10)$. Its slope is $\dfrac{10-0}{5-0}=2$ m/s$^2$ — matching the acceleration exactly. The area under that line, a triangle with base $5$ and height $10$, is $\tfrac{1}{2}(5)(10)=25$ m — matching the displacement found above.
