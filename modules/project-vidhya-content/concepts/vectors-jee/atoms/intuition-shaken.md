Start with the dot product formula: $\vec a\cdot\vec b=|\vec a||\vec b|\cos\theta$.

Think of it as a shadow. Shine a light straight down onto the direction of $\vec b$. Vector $\vec a$ casts a shadow of length $|\vec a|\cos\theta$ on that direction. Multiply the shadow length by $|\vec b|$ — that product is the dot product.

If $\vec a$ points the same way as $\vec b$, the shadow is at its longest, and the dot product is at its largest. If $\vec a$ is perpendicular to $\vec b$, the shadow shrinks to nothing — the dot product is exactly $0$.

Now the cross product: $\vec a\times\vec b=|\vec a||\vec b|\sin\theta\,\hat n$. The size part, $|\vec a||\vec b|\sin\theta$, is the area of the parallelogram with $\vec a$ and $\vec b$ as two sides. Same direction for both vectors means the parallelogram is squashed flat — area $0$.

One more use: for two points with position vectors $\vec a$ and $\vec b$, the midpoint is $\frac{\vec a+\vec b}{2}$. That single fact proves whole geometry results without any coordinates.
