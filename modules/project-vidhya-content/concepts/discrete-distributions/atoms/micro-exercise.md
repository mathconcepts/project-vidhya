$X\sim\text{Poisson}(\lambda=2)$. Find $P(X=0)$.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$P(X=0) = \dfrac{e^{-2}\cdot2^0}{0!} = e^{-2} \approx 0.1353$.
</details>
