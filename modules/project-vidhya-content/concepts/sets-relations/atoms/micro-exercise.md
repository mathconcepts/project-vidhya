On $\{1,2,3,4\}$, let $aRb \iff a$ and $b$ have the same parity. How many equivalence classes does $R$ have?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Two: $\{1,3\}$ (odd) and $\{2,4\}$ (even). Same-parity is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive.
</details>
