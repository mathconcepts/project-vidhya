Find the variance of the data $2,4,6,8,10$.

- **(A)** $6$
- **(B)** $8$
- **(C)** $40$
- **(D)** $2.83$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. Mean $\bar x=\dfrac{2+4+6+8+10}{5}=\dfrac{30}{5}=6$.

Deviations from the mean: $-4,-2,0,2,4$. Squares: $16,4,0,4,16$. Sum $=40$.

$$\sigma^2=\frac{40}{5}=8$$

(Option A is the mean, not the variance. Option C is the sum of squared deviations, not divided by $n$ yet. Option D is the standard deviation, $\sqrt8\approx2.83$, not the variance.)

</details>
