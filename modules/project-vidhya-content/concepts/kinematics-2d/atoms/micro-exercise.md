A stone tied to a string of length $r=0.5$ m is whirled in a horizontal circle at a constant speed $v=4$ m/s. Find its centripetal acceleration.

A) $32$ m/s$^2$
B) $16$ m/s$^2$
C) $8$ m/s$^2$
D) $64$ m/s$^2$
E) $2$ m/s$^2$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct answer: A) $32$ m/s$^2$.**

$a_c = \dfrac{v^2}{r} = \dfrac{4^2}{0.5} = \dfrac{16}{0.5}=32$ m/s$^2$, directed from the stone toward the centre of the circle.

**Why the others fail:**
- B) $16$ m/s$^2$ comes from $\dfrac{v^2}{2r}$ — a formula from straight-line motion (like $s=v^2/2a$), wrongly borrowed here.
- C) $8$ m/s$^2$ comes from $v/r$, forgetting to square the speed at all.
- D) $64$ m/s$^2$ comes from $\dfrac{2v^2}{r}$ — doubling the correct value by mistake.
- E) $2$ m/s$^2$ comes from $v \times r$, using multiplication where division was needed.

</details>
