**Question:** State Bernoulli's equation, the assumptions it needs, and the excess-pressure formula for a soap bubble versus a plain liquid drop.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Bernoulli's equation:** $P+\tfrac12\rho v^2+\rho gh = \text{constant}$ along a streamline, requiring steady, incompressible, non-viscous flow.

**Excess pressure — drop:** $\Delta P = 2T/r$. **Soap bubble** (two surfaces): $\Delta P = 4T/r$.

</details>
