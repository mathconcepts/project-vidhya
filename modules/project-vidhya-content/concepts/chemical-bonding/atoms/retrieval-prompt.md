**Question:** State the formal charge formula, and the bond order formula from molecular orbital theory. Then state, in one line each, what question VSEPR, hybridisation, and MO theory each answer.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Formal charge:** $\text{FC} = V - L - \dfrac{B}{2}$ (valence electrons minus lone-pair electrons minus half the bonding electrons).

**Bond order:** $\dfrac{\text{bonding electrons} - \text{antibonding electrons}}{2}$.

- **VSEPR** answers: what is the molecule's *shape*?
- **Hybridisation** answers: what *orbital mixture* on the central atom produces that shape?
- **MO theory** answers: what is the *bond order*, and how many electrons are *unpaired*?

</details>
