Picture a long line of dominoes, but a strange kind: knocking over an "electric" domino doesn't knock over the next electric domino — it knocks over a "magnetic" domino standing right next to it, at a right angle. That magnetic domino falling then knocks over the *next* electric domino further down the line, which knocks over another magnetic domino beside it, and so on, endlessly, with no one pushing after the very first tap.

This is how an electromagnetic wave keeps itself going through empty space with no medium at all: a changing electric field creates a changing magnetic field beside it (Maxwell's fix from the hook), and that changing magnetic field, by Faraday's law, creates a changing electric field further along — each one regenerating the other, perpendicular to each other and to the direction the whole chain is falling, forever, at a fixed speed $c$.
