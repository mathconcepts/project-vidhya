**Unit cell**: the smallest repeating box that, stacked in three dimensions, builds the entire crystal.

**Atoms per unit cell ($Z$)**, found by counting shares: a corner atom contributes $\frac{1}{8}$ (shared among $8$ cells), a face-centred atom contributes $\frac{1}{2}$ (shared between $2$ cells), an edge atom contributes $\frac{1}{4}$ (shared among $4$ cells), a body-centred atom contributes $1$ (belongs entirely to one cell).

**Simple cubic**: $Z=1$, atoms touch along the edge, $a=2r$, packing efficiency $\dfrac{\pi}{6}\approx52.4\%$, coordination number $6$.

**Body-centred cubic (bcc)**: $Z=2$, atoms touch along the body diagonal, $\sqrt{3}a=4r$, packing efficiency $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}\pi}{8}\approx68\%$, coordination number $8$.

**Face-centred cubic (fcc / cubic close-packed)**: $Z=4$, atoms touch along a face diagonal, $\sqrt{2}a=4r$, packing efficiency $\dfrac{\pi}{3\sqrt{2}}\approx74\%$, coordination number $12$ — the maximum packing efficiency possible for identical spheres.

**Density of a unit cell**: $\rho=\dfrac{ZM}{N_Aa^3}$, where $M$ is molar mass, $N_A$ is Avogadro's number, and $a$ is the edge length, converted to centimetres before cubing so density comes out in $\text{g/cm}^3$.

**Voids in a close-packed structure**: for $N$ atoms, there are $N$ octahedral voids (coordination number $6$) and $2N$ tetrahedral voids (coordination number $4$).

**Point defects**: a **Schottky defect** (a cation-anion pair both missing, keeping the crystal electrically neutral, seen in ionic solids of similarly-sized ions, and LOWERS density) and a **Frenkel defect** (a smaller ion displaced from its normal site into an interstitial gap, seen when the two ions differ greatly in size, and does NOT change density).
