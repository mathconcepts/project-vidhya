Two forces pull a ring in different directions at once: one force of 5 N due east, another of 5 N at 60° north of east. Do the forces add like plain numbers — giving 10 N — or does the ring feel something smaller? Vectors carry direction as well as size, and adding two of them means completing a parallelogram, not stacking two lengths on a number line. The net pull here works out to less than 10 N, and exactly how much less is one dot product away.
