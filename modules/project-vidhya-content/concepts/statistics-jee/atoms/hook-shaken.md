City A's rainfall over $5$ years: $100,102,98,101,99$. City B's: $100,80,120,90,110$.

Add City A's numbers: $100+102+98+101+99=500$. Divide by $5$: mean $=100$.

Add City B's numbers: $100+80+120+90+110=500$. Divide by $5$: mean $=100$.

Same mean, $100$, for both cities. But look at how far each year strays from $100$. City A: $0,2,-2,1,-1$ — small strays. City B: $0,-20,20,-10,10$ — big strays. The mean alone hides this completely. A number that measures HOW FAR values typically stray from the mean is what this concept is about.
