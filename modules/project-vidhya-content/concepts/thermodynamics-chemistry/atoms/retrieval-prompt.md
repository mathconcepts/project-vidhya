**Question:** State the Gibbs free energy formula and the spontaneity condition. Then state chemistry's sign convention for $q$ and $w$ in the first law, and how it differs from the physics convention.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$. $\Delta G<0$: spontaneous. $\Delta G=0$: equilibrium. $\Delta G>0$: non-spontaneous.

**Chemistry (IUPAC):** $\Delta U = q+w$, with $w$ = work done *on* the system (positive on compression).

**Physics:** often $\Delta U=q-w$, with $w$ = work done *by* the system (positive on expansion) — the opposite sign for $w$.

</details>
