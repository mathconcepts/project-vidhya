**Question:** State the formula for gravitational potential energy (with its sign convention), and the formula for escape velocity in terms of $g$ and $R$.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Gravitational potential energy:** $U=-\dfrac{GMm}{r}$, taking $U=0$ at $r=\infty$. Always negative for finite $r$.

**Escape velocity:** $v_e=\sqrt{\dfrac{2GM}{R}} = \sqrt{2gR}$ (using $GM=gR^2$).

</details>
