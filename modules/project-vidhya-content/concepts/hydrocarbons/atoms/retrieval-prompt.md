**Question:** State all four conditions Huckel's rule requires for a ring to be aromatic, and state what an electron count of exactly $4n$ (instead of $4n+2$) means for a ring that meets the other three conditions.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The ring must be: (1) cyclic, (2) planar (flat), (3) fully conjugated (a p-orbital on every ring atom, with no gap), and (4) carrying exactly $4n+2$ pi electrons for some whole number $n$.

A ring meeting conditions (1)-(3) but carrying exactly $4n$ pi electrons is **antiaromatic** — less stable than an equivalent open-chain molecule, not more.

</details>
