**"Order is Observed, Molecularity is Mechanism."** Both start differently on purpose: Order comes from data (Observed); Molecularity comes from a proposed Mechanism. Never let one stand in for the other.

**"First order never forgets its half-life."** Whatever the starting concentration, a first-order reaction always takes the SAME time to halve — the half-life "forgets" how much was there to begin with.

**"$0.693$ is $\ln 2$, memorised as one number."** $t_{1/2}=0.693/k$ for first order — treat $0.693$ as a single constant to recall, rather than recomputing $\ln 2$ from scratch under time pressure.
