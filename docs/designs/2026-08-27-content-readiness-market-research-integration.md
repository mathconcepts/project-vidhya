## 0. What this plan responds to

Adverse feedback named three things: **content readiness**, **content
preparation**, and **content handling structure**. The user commissioned an
independent market study (177-file corpus: pain-point research, a 116-topic
atomic inventory with a validated prerequisite graph, a research-first
content-generation framework with schemas and policies, 106+10 remediation
workflow contracts, a constraint/observability framework, a manim animation
skill, and official GATE XE 2021–2023 paper analysis). The job: compare it
against Vidhya, adopt what is better, and produce a rock-solid improvement
strategy. GATE Engineering Mathematics is the pilot; IIT JEE mathematics
follows.

**Prior art in this repo (must not be re-litigated):** the 2026-08-25
intent-driven restructure plan already answered the *first* round of this
feedback. Its Phases 1–3 shipped: the 203-atom demand catalogue
(`data/curriculum/gate-em/atomic-catalogue.json` — same research lineage as
this corpus's `GATE_Engineering_Mathematics_Atomic_Catalogue.csv`), intent
profiles, the CI catalogue gate, the problem-statement block, and
intent-aware blueprints. Open tasks from that plan: lane chips (T4b),
inventory floor + generation (T6), ErrorTag extension + trap drills (T7),
cross-paper packs (T8). This plan **absorbs T6/T7 and re-specs them** with
the stronger material from the new corpus; T4b and T8 are unchanged.

## 3. Premises (the final gate confirms these)

1. **The feedback is about exam-craft depth and preparation governance, not
   about the floor.** Every concept already walks all four legs; the demo
   critique survives because content is thin (~5 items/concept vs 43 target)
   and does not yet *teach the exam* (mode, time, risk) or *prove its
   preparation* (provenance, gates).
2. **The market study is a spec library, not a content library.** Its 114
   packages are generation contracts stamped `candidate_pending_review`; the
   adoptable value is governance, anatomy, and the remediation/diagnosis
   protocol — not lessons to import.
3. **Vidhya's engine + measurement stack is the keeper.** Nothing in the
   corpus replaces Elo/FSRS/FIRe/lift; everything adopted lands as data,
   policy, or thin modules on existing seams.
4. **The concept graph remains the single mastery spine.** The study's
   116-topic graph is a validation and enrichment source (typed edges,
   missing-edge candidates), never a second runtime taxonomy. (Same D2/D5
   dual-key decision as the 2026-08-25 plan — reaffirmed, not reopened.)
5. **GATE pilot → JEE follows.** Every adopted mechanism must be keyed by
   `(exam, paper, year)` contract data, not GATE constants. The Assessment
   Contract (W1.1) is the portability proof: JEE lands as a data row + syllabus
   pack, zero scorer forks.

## 4. The strategy — three workstreams matching the three feedback words

### W1. Content PREPARATION → the Governance Contract

*Make every claim, mark, and generated artifact provably prepared.*

- **W1.1 Versioned Assessment Contract** (migration `0XX_assessment_contracts` +
  loader + seed row from the corpus's verified GATE 2026 facts: MCQ −1/3|−2/3,
  MSQ no-partial no-negative, NAT no-negative + tolerance, EM = 13 marks,
  XE-A = 15). `GateDeterministicScorer` reads marking params from the contract
  (same math, new source of truth); every attempt row stamps
  `contract_version`. JEE = a second row.
- **W1.2 Evidence labels.** `evidence_level: official | directly_reviewed |
  pattern_supported | design_hypothesis` on PYQ pattern claims and atom
  exam-relevance statements; D/P/S codes imported from the corpus's
  historical-pattern map for the 116 topics; CI rule: no "high-yield /
  frequently-asked" copy without `directly_reviewed` or a coded corpus.
- **W1.3 Quality-gate ledger.** Per generated curriculum unit / practice-item
  batch: named gate statuses (scope, mathematics, assessment-contract,
  misconception-coverage, provenance) persisted alongside the existing
  `canonical` lifecycle; `automatic_release_forbidden_for` = answer keys,
  marking rules, canonical definitions — these always require operator
  approval (matches existing admin-approval surfaces).
- **W1.4 Regression-case bank.** Wrong answer keys, misrouted remediations,
  demoted experiments → stored regression cases; generation-prompt or policy
  version bumps replay the bank before the ledger may promote.
- **W1.5 Syllabus freshness watch.** Hash the official GATE syllabus/pattern
  pages (the corpus carries the 2026 URLs); on change, flag affected packs —
  a scheduler job + one table, alert-only.
- **W1.6 Anti-gaming guards in the ledger.** Promote blocked (→ operator
  review) when: immediate lift with flat delayed retention, MCQ accuracy up
  while NAT down, time down while errors up. Pure-function checks over data
  the ledger already reads.

### W2. Content HANDLING STRUCTURE → the Package Anatomy

*One canonical anatomy for every concept package; personalization attaches,
never rewrites.*

- **W2.1 Template families.** Enrich `template-engine.ts`'s 3-way mapping to
  the corpus's 14 families (eigen: geometric_visual → characteristic_eq;
  ode: classification → method_decision_tree; …) expressed in the existing
  StageKind/AtomKind vocabulary + `presentation` hints. Family table is
  data (`data/curriculum/gate-em/template-families.yml`), codegen'd like
  intent-tables.
- **W2.2 Anchor IDs.** Stage instances get stable anchor ids in the
  blueprint→unit translation, so deltas and A/B variants attach to a named
  position instead of a whole atom. (Additive: `to-unit-spec.ts` output
  metadata; no locked-contract mutation.)
- **W2.3 Anchored deltas (phase 2 of stance system).** New variant grain:
  `misconception_contrast`, `prerequisite_bridge`, `timed_variant` deltas
  attached to anchors, composed at serve time; conflict with base content →
  refuse + review queue. Stance variants remain the representation-preference
  mechanism; deltas are the remediation mechanism. Delta-level lift lands in
  the existing experiments machinery (`target_kind` already accepts atoms).
- **W2.4 Composition manifest.** Log which base version + deltas + routing
  reason a session was served (aggregate-safe: keyed by session like existing
  attempt rows). Prerequisite for debugging personalization and honest
  delta-cohort lift.
- **W2.5 `decision_tree` interactive kind (4th widget).** Method-selection
  trainer: node = question about the problem (region closed? boundary
  oriented?), leaf = method + reason code. Ships with VC theorem selection,
  distribution choice, PI-template choice, rank/consistency. Dependency-free
  like the other three, versioned spec (`v: 1`).
- **W2.6 Checkpoint-branched lesson flow.** Lesson composer inserts a
  checkpoint after worked_example; wrong choice routes to the matching
  misconception_contrast delta (W2.3), right choice proceeds. No video
  runtime — existing atoms + widgets.

### W3. Content READINESS → Depth with Exam-Craft

*Close the 505 → 8,725 gap in leverage order, and make the content teach the
exam, not just the math.*

- **W3.1 Mode-readiness dimensions.** Per (concept, mode ∈ MCQ/MSQ/NAT):
  detect mode gaps from existing attempt data (correct-MCQ + wrong-NAT
  patterns). Two added state dimensions (mode, fluency/time) — not the full
  9-dim matrix. Feeds nextBestAction as modifiers, not a new engine.
- **W3.2 Risk & attempt-discipline training.** Post-mock counterfactual
  ("attempting Q17 cost you 0.67; skipping earns +2 EV at your accuracy") from
  mock-to-marks' existing leftOnTable; a 5-item attempt/skip drill mode using
  break-even p = P/(R+P) from the contract (W1.1). NAT numeric-entry
  discipline items (tolerance/rounding traps) tagged as such.
- **W3.3 Remediation workflow contracts.** Data-driven state machine:
  trigger (two-signal) → diagnostic probe → smallest bridge → immediate check
  → scheduled delayed check (FSRS already does this) → transfer check →
  exit state; escalate to operator after 2 failed cycles; diagnostic budget
  (480s default / 240s crunch-phase). Pilot with the corpus's two
  highest-leverage fully-specified workflows: **LA-05 consistency** (rank
  decision tree bridge) and **VC-11 theorem selection** (decision-tree widget
  from W2.5). The remaining 8 top-10 workflows follow as data files once the
  machine is proven.
- **W3.4 Failure taxonomy → ErrorTag extension (absorbs T7).** Migration
  extends the locked union with the corpus taxonomy mapped to concrete tags:
  `method_selection`, `representation`, `mode_msq`, `mode_nat_entry`,
  `time_pressure`, `risk_decision`, `prerequisite` (+ existing 6). Distractor
  generation (`deriveMarking` + generation prompts) requires each MCQ
  distractor to name its failure hypothesis — a wrong pick becomes a
  diagnosis.
- **W3.5 Inventory fill (absorbs T6).** Floor gate reads per-atom
  `question_inventory` targets (report-only → per-module ratchet). Batch
  generation runs ordered by the corpus's leverage list (LA-05, PS-03, VC-11,
  CA-21, CA-13, NM-08, NM-09, PS-04, DE-05, CA-18 → then module sweeps), each
  run's spec carrying: mode mix (≈40/30/30 MCQ/MSQ/NAT per catalogue),
  misconception-linked distractors (W3.4), MSQ complete-truth-set
  verification, NAT tolerance authored per contract. Verification through the
  existing 3-tier cascade; items land only via the existing gates.
- **W3.6 Media QA loop.** Post-render frame extraction (mid + end frame per
  GIF) + LLM legibility/overlap check → `media_artifacts.status='failed'` on
  fail (existing skip machinery). Applies to the 70 rendered scenes and all
  future renders.
- **W3.7 Confidence & calibration.** Optional one-tap confidence before
  reveal on practice attempts; calibration error (|confidence − accuracy|)
  as an aggregate metric and a ledger protected metric. Per-attempt field on
  existing attempt rows; no new per-student profile (surveillance-invariant
  compatible).
- **W3.8 Triage & re-entry session modes.** Crunch-phase triage that *shows
  deferrals* ("deferring Jordan forms — 2 marks at risk, 9 concepts stronger
  first") and a no-shame re-entry path after ≥7-day absence. Policy layer on
  `SyllabusAwareReadinessEngine` + a session-plan surface change.

## 8b. Implementation alternatives considered (0C-bis)

**APPROACH A — Content-only sprint.** Skip governance/anatomy; pour everything
into batch-generating the 8,220 missing items now.
- Effort: M | Risk: High
- Pros: fastest visible depth; uses existing pipeline unchanged.
- Cons: generates against the current anatomy (no misconception-linked
  distractors, no mode mix contract, no gate ledger) — thousands of items
  that would need regeneration once the anatomy locks; repeats the exact
  "volume without preparation" failure the feedback names.

**APPROACH B — Governance first, then anatomy, then volume (chosen).**
Adopt the study's contract/provenance/anatomy layers as data + thin modules
on existing seams, then fill inventory in leverage order against the locked
anatomy.
- Effort: L (spread over 7 shippable phases) | Risk: Medium
- Pros: every generated item lands provably prepared; JEE portability proven
  at P1; each phase independently shippable and reversible.
- Cons: depth arrives in P3, not immediately; more moving parts than A.

**APPROACH C — Adopt the study's stack wholesale.** Implement its schemas
(claims registry, 13 agents, 9-dim state, base/delta DB) as designed.
- Effort: XL | Risk: High
- Pros: maximum fidelity to the research.
- Cons: duplicates working Vidhya subsystems (experiments, FSRS, verifiers);
  the study's own feasibility audit warns against exactly this; a one-operator
  platform cannot review 13 agent pipelines; months of rebuild before any
  student-visible change.

**Decision: B.** A is the trap the feedback already caught; C fails the
study's own audit. B is A's output quality with C's discipline, at a
fraction of C's machinery.

## 9. Dream state delta

Today: a mastery engine with a green-but-thin content floor that grades the
exam but does not teach exam-craft, generated by a pipeline that cannot prove
its preparation. After P1–P5: every content package has one canonical
anatomy, a provenance label, and a gate ledger; every mark traces to a
versioned exam contract; wrong answers trigger bounded, evidence-gated
remediation; and the inventory grows in leverage order with
misconception-linked distractors. JEE onboarding = one contract row + one
syllabus pack (P7) — the pilot's promise kept structurally, not aspirationally.
