For $\mathbf F(x,y,z)=(x^2,y^2,z^2)$, find $\operatorname{div}\mathbf F$ at $(1,1,1)$ and $\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F$.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$\operatorname{div}\mathbf F=2x+2y+2z$; at $(1,1,1)$: $2+2+2=6$. $\operatorname{curl}\mathbf F=(0,0,0)$ — each component depends only on its own variable, so every cross-partial in the curl formula is zero.
</details>
