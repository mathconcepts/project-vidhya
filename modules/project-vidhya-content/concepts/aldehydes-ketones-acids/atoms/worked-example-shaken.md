**Problem:** Acetaldehyde ($\text{CH}_3\text{CHO}$) is treated with dilute NaOH. Name the reaction and the final product.

---

**Step 1 — Look at the carbon next to the carbonyl carbon.** In $\text{CH}_3\text{CHO}$ that is the $\text{CH}_3$ carbon. Count its hydrogens: three.

---

**Step 2 — Three hydrogens present means this molecule HAS alpha-hydrogens.** So base removes one of them, forming an enolate. Cannizzaro needs zero alpha-hydrogens, so it is ruled out here — the reaction is aldol, not Cannizzaro.

---

**Step 3 — The enolate attacks a second, untouched $\text{CH}_3\text{CHO}$ molecule.** A new carbon-carbon bond forms between them.

---

**Step 4 — Protonate the result.** Product: $\text{CH}_3\text{-CH(OH)-CH}_2\text{-CHO}$, called 3-hydroxybutanal.

---

**Step 5 — Heat it: it loses one water molecule.** The $\text{-OH}$ and one neighbouring hydrogen leave as $\text{H}_2\text{O}$, forming a $\text{C=C}$ bond next to the carbonyl.

$$\boxed{\text{CH}_3\text{-CH=CH-CHO (crotonaldehyde)}}$$

**Check:** count carbons in the product — four. Two molecules of $\text{CH}_3\text{CHO}$ (two carbons each) joined by one new bond gives four carbons total. Matches.
