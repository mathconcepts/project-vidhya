Two differential equations can share every symbol on the page and still demand opposite solution techniques — the difference is invisible until you classify them first. Before touching a single method, GATE expects three quick reads on any ODE: how many times has it been differentiated (its **order**), what power sits on that top derivative once the equation is cleared of roots and fractions (its **degree** — sometimes there isn't one), and does it stay **linear** in $y$ and every derivative? Get any of the three wrong and you'll reach for separation of variables on an equation that actually needs an integrating factor — or isn't solvable by either.
