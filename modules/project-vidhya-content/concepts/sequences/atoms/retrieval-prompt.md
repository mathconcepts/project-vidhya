Before checking, try to recall from memory: what two conditions, together, guarantee a sequence converges — without knowing its limit in advance — even though neither condition alone is enough?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>Bounded **and** monotonic (the Monotone Convergence Theorem). Either alone can fail: $(-1)^n$ is bounded but not monotonic; $a_n=n$ is monotonic but not bounded. Neither converges.</details>
