A mass sits at position $2$ m, moving at $3$ m/s. Position alone, $2$, doesn't say what happens next — the speed $3$ is a second, independent number the equation needs. Two numbers, so the equation is second order. Bolt on a damper and a third number joins the list, and the order climbs again.
