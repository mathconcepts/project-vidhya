**Question:** State the four levels of protein structure. Then state which one describes just the amino acid sequence, and which describes the full folded three-dimensional shape.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**The four levels:** primary, secondary, tertiary, quaternary.

**Primary structure** is just the sequence — the order in which amino acids are joined along the chain, nothing more.

**Tertiary structure** is the full three-dimensional folded shape the whole chain settles into.

(Secondary structure is local folding patterns, like the alpha helix, held together by hydrogen bonds; quaternary structure is multiple folded chains assembled together, as in haemoglobin's four subunits.)

</details>
