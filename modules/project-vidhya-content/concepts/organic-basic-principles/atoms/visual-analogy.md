Picture a bucket-brigade of people passing water hand to hand toward a fire — that is the **inductive effect**. Each person passes a little less than they received, so by the fourth or fifth pair of hands there is almost nothing left. The effect is real but it dies out fast, because it only ever moves along one link at a time.

Now picture a crowd standing in a fully connected circle, arms linked at the elbow, all of them free to lean their weight toward one spot at once — that is the **resonance effect**. Because everyone is genuinely joined into one connected loop, weight (electron density) can shift all the way to the far side of the circle in one go — something the single-file bucket brigade could never do, because it has no loop to lean around.

**Hyperconjugation** is a bystander standing right next to an empty chair (an empty orbital) and quietly sliding a spare cushion (an electron pair from a neighbouring $\text{C-H}$ bond) into it, without anyone officially "handing it over" — the cushion just overlaps into the gap. The more empty-chair neighbours a spot has, the more cushions can slide in, and the more comfortable that spot becomes.
