# Teaching Tips: Product & Quotient Rule

## Common Student Errors

- **Wrong order in product rule:** Students write $(u'v)$ instead of $(u'v + uv')$.
- **Quotient rule denominator:** Students forget to square $v$ in the denominator or mess up the sign (should be $u'v - uv'$, not $uv' - u'v$).
- **Not simplifying:** Students leave factorization opportunities, making the answer look wrong.

## GATE Question Pattern

GATE asks: find $f'(x)$ for product/quotient (MCQ or NAT). Typical: $x e^x$, $x \sin(x)$, $\frac{x}{e^x}$. Combined with chain rule in higher-difficulty questions. Often 1–2 marks.

## Speed Tricks for MCQs

- **Product rule mnemonic:** "First times derivative of second, plus second times derivative of first."
- **Quotient rule mnemonic:** "Low d-high minus high d-low, square the bottom and away we go."
- **Simplify after:** Always factor and simplify before submitting.

## Must-Memorize Formulas / Results

- **Product rule:** $(uv)' = u'v + uv'$
- **Quotient rule:** $(u/v)' = (u'v - uv')/v^2$
