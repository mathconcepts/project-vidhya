- **Imagining displacement current as real charge crossing the gap.** No charge ever moves between a capacitor's plates. Displacement current is a changing electric field, counted *as if* it were a current so that Ampere's law gives the same answer for every surface — it produces a real magnetic field, but it is not made of moving charge.

- **Assuming $E$ and $B$ are $90^\circ$ out of phase in an electromagnetic wave**, by analogy with the voltage-current phase lag from AC circuits. They are not: in a plane electromagnetic wave, $E$ and $B$ oscillate **in phase** — both zero together, both at their peak together.

- **Believing electromagnetic waves need a medium to travel through**, the way sound needs air. They do not; unlike a mechanical wave, an electromagnetic wave is a self-sustaining pattern of changing $E$ and $B$ fields and travels through vacuum at $c$.

- **Mixing up $p=U/c$ (full absorption) with $p=2U/c$ (full reflection).** A perfectly reflecting surface receives *twice* the momentum of a perfectly absorbing one for the same incident energy $U$, since the wave's momentum reverses direction rather than simply vanishing.

- **Treating the electromagnetic spectrum's order as something to memorise by wavelength size alone, unconnected to frequency.** Wavelength and frequency move in *opposite* directions along the spectrum — radio waves have the longest wavelength and lowest frequency, gamma rays the shortest wavelength and highest frequency — so a question phrased in terms of frequency needs the order read backwards from one phrased in terms of wavelength.
