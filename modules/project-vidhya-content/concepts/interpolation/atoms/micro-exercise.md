Given $(0,1)$ and $(1,3)$, find the interpolating polynomial and evaluate it at $x=0.5$.

- **(A)** $P(0.5)=1.5$
- **(B)** $P(0.5)=2$
- **(C)** $P(0.5)=2.5$
- **(D)** $P(0.5)=3$

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**B**. Two points give a line: $P(x)=1(1-x)+3x=1+2x$. $P(0.5)=1+2(0.5)=2$.

</details>
