**How JEE actually asks this.**

- **NAT: direct $\Delta G$, or crossover temperature, from given $\Delta H$ and $\Delta S$.** Convert units first (kJ vs. J), then substitute — or, for a crossover temperature, set $\Delta G=0$ and solve $T=\Delta H/\Delta S$ directly.

- **MCQ/NAT: applying Hess's Law with two or three given step-reactions.** Add or reverse the given equations (flipping a reaction's direction flips the sign of its $\Delta H$) so intermediate species cancel, matching the target reaction exactly, then add the corresponding $\Delta H$ values.

- **Trap: unit mismatch between $\Delta H$ (kJ) and $\Delta S$ (J) is deliberately built into the numbers.** A question with "nice" numbers in both units is testing exactly whether the factor-of-1000 conversion is remembered.

- **Trap: sign convention for $w$.** If the question is phrased using "work done by the gas", it is very likely importing physics's convention — check which formula the question intends before substituting into $\Delta U=q+w$.

- **Time budget:** a direct $\Delta G$ NAT should take under 45 seconds once units are converted. A multi-step Hess's Law question needs closer to 90 seconds to correctly orient and add the given equations.
