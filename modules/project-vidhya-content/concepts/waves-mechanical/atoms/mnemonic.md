**"The pattern runs, the particle bounces."** The wave's pattern runs steadily forward at $v=f\lambda$. Each particle it passes through only ever bounces up and down in place — never runs anywhere.

**"Approach subtracts below, recede adds below"** — for the source term sitting in the Doppler formula's denominator. A source closing in makes the bottom smaller (so the fraction, and the heard pitch, grows); a source pulling away makes the bottom bigger (so pitch falls).

**"Odd end closed, all ends open"** for organ pipes: a pipe with one end sealed shut plays only ODD harmonics; a pipe open at both ends plays ALL of them.
