A matrix norm answers one question: what's the most this matrix can stretch a length-one arrow? Push every direction through and most arrows land somewhere in between, but two directions matter most — the one that grows the most, and the one that grows the least. The matrix norm is simply the biggest of those two: the longest reach of the stretched circle, now an ellipse, in any direction at all.
