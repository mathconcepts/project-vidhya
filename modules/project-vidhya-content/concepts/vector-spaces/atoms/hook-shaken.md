Take $(1,2)$ and $(3,-1)$ in $\mathbb{R}^2$. Add them: $(4,1)$ — still in $\mathbb{R}^2$. Scale $(1,2)$ by $5$: $(5,10)$ — still there too.

That's the whole idea. A vector space is a set where adding two members, or scaling one, never leaves the set.
