$x^2+y^2=1$ is a circle — closed, same shape all the way round. That's a positive definite form's level curve: a bowl, opening up everywhere.

$x^2-y^2=1$ is a hyperbola — opens outward, never closes. That's indefinite: positive some directions, negative others — a saddle, not a bowl.

The eigenvalues of $A$ decide which picture you get. Check their signs; skip the plotting.
