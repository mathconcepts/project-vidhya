**Question:** State the formula for the magnetic force on a moving charge, the angle it depends on, and the two special angles where the force vanishes.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$F=qvB\sin\theta$, where $\theta$ is the angle between the velocity $\vec v$ and the field $\vec B$.

The force is exactly zero when $\theta=0°$ (velocity parallel to the field) or $\theta=180°$ (velocity antiparallel to the field) — regardless of how large $q$, $v$, or $B$ are.

Direction (positive charge): right-hand rule, curling from $\vec v$ towards $\vec B$. Flip the direction for a negative charge.

</details>
