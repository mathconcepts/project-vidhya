Compute $\det \begin{pmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$.

- **(A)** 10
- **(B)** 11
- **(C)** 12
- **(D)** 14

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**A**. $\det\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} = ad - bc = 3(4) - 2(1) = 12 - 2 = 10$.

</details>
