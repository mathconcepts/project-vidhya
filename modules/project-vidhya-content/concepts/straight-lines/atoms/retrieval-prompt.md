From memory, before checking: $L_1+\lambda L_2=0$ gives you a whole family of lines through one fixed point for free. What extra piece of information do you still need, to pin down ONE specific value of $\lambda$?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

One more condition on the line you actually want — that it passes through a given point, or runs parallel to a given line, or runs perpendicular to one. Substitute that condition into $L_1+\lambda L_2=0$, solve the resulting equation for $\lambda$, and you have your one specific line — still without ever solving $L_1=0$ and $L_2=0$ together.

</details>
