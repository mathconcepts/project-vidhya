Determine whether $\int_1^\infty \frac{1}{x^3}\,dx$ converges, and if so, find its value.

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$p=3>1$ at infinity, so it converges. $\int_1^N x^{-3}dx=\left[-\tfrac{1}{2}x^{-2}\right]_1^N=\tfrac12-\tfrac{1}{2N^2}$. As $N\to\infty$, this $\to\tfrac12$.

</details>
